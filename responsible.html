Responsible play: the TipsyPlay house code

TipsyPlay is about laughs, not quantity. A cocktail, a soft drink, water, or no drink — alcohol is never required.

You must be of legal drinking age in your location.

The house code

House policy. Every turn is opt-in. Pass any time, without explanation. You may decline both a drink and any alternative — you do not owe a replacement action.

Controls differ from game to game. Some screens have a Pass control; some do not. The right to decline still stands either way. If a screen only offers actions someone has declined, pause or stop instead of recording a choice they did not make. Never push anyone to drink, pose, share a secret, or keep playing.

Where a game mentions a sip, keep it one small optional sip. Prefer water or skip entirely. Do not carry drink penalties into later rounds. No chugging, no drinking races, and no pressure to finish a drink. Timers are for the game — never a reason to race a drink.

This page is not a medical or safety guarantee.

Water and food on the table. Set them out before the first round and leave them there. That is a host job.

Never drink and drive. Plan a ride, a designated driver, or a place to stay before you start.

How our games handle it

Alcohol-free play belongs at the same table.

Rules pages mention non-alcoholic play. Use the house policy above whenever you want to decline.

Split Decision, Vibe Checkpoint, and Chaos Crew keep score in points. Cup Toss Challenge is also scored in points, and its instructions say nobody drinks from the game cups.

Where a game mentions a sip — including King's Cup and Card Lottery — keep it optional and small. Water and soft drinks count. Passing and alcohol-free play remain full ways to take part.

Tips for hosts

Say the house policy out loud before round one: decline a drink and any alternative, no explanation, no replacement.

Agree on house rules first. See friendly house rules for card drinking games.

Pick a game that fits. Choose a party game for your group, or plan an alcohol-free party game night.

Make stopping easy. End after a set number of rounds, and check in before starting another.

Need help?

If you or someone you know is struggling with alcohol, talk with a doctor or a local health service. TipsyPlay does not give medical care, counseling, or emergency help. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

In Canada, the Canadian Centre on Substance Use and Addiction publishes Canada's Guidance on Alcohol and Health. That page is health guidance for people in Canada — not a helpline.

In the United States, the SAMHSA National Helpline at 1-800-662-4357 is a free, confidential, 24/7 treatment referral and information service in English and Spanish. It does not provide counseling, and it is not emergency help.
