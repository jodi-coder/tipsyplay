Responsible play 💙

TipsyPlay is about laughs, not quantity. Here's the house code:

You must be of legal drinking age in your location.

🙋 Every game is opt-in. Pass on any turn, any time.

🧠 Know your limits. Pace yourself; sips are optional and small.

💧 Drink water between rounds. Our games remind you.

🚗 Never drink and drive. Plan a ride, a designated driver, or a place to crash.

🧃 Non-alcoholic is always fine. Every game has an alternative.

🤝 No pressure. Never push anyone to drink or share.

🍕 Eat something before and during.

Need help?

If you or someone you know is struggling with alcohol, reach out to a doctor, a local health service, or a national alcohol support helpline in your country. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
