About this game

Category Countdown is a timed naming game with two modes. In Spotlight, one player names as many answers as they can in 30 seconds while everyone else can challenge. In Hot Potato, everyone shares one category and adds an answer each until only one player is left. Repeats are caught automatically, and every drink has a no-drink alternative.

TipsyPlay runs in the browser — no app download. One person hosts a room, friends join with a short code, and every phone stays in sync. Drinking is always optional. Non-alcoholic alternatives (points, poses, tallies, or challenges) are a full way to play.

Keep the night beginner-friendly: read the on-screen prompt, take one small optional drink at most when a round calls for it, and never stack penalties. If someone wants to pass, that is always fine. Plan a ride home before anyone drinks.

Browse more games for adults on the games list, including card drinking games like King's Cup and Card Lottery.

Hosting tips: name yourself something the table recognizes, share the code out loud once, and mute sound effects if you are in a quiet building. Guests can leave anytime without closing the room for everyone else.

Responsible play: you must be of legal drinking age in your location. There is no chugging, no races, and no pressure to finish a drink. Soft drinks and mocktails are first-class options on every TipsyPlay table.
