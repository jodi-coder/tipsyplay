    About this game

    Cup Toss Challenge scores hits into water-filled cups. Points only from the game cups. Winner can pick the next song.

    TipsyPlay runs in the browser — no app download. One person hosts a room, friends join with a short code, and every phone stays in sync. Sipping is always optional. Water counts. Non-alcoholic alternatives (points, poses, tallies, or challenges) are a full way to play.

    Keep the night beginner-friendly: read the on-screen prompt, take one small optional sip at most when a round calls for it, and never stack penalties. If someone wants to pass, that is always fine. Plan a ride home before anyone drinks.

    Browse more games for adults on the games list, including card drinking games like King's Cup and Card Lottery.

    Hosting tips: name yourself something the table recognizes, share the code out loud once, and mute sound effects if you are in a quiet building. Guests can leave anytime without closing the room for everyone else.

    Responsible play: you must be of legal drinking age in your location. There is no chugging, no races, and no pressure to finish a drink. Soft drinks, mocktails, and water are first-class options on every TipsyPlay table.
