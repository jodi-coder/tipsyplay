    How Slow Drip works

    The host opens a room and friends join on their own phones. A phrase shows as blanks, with a few letters already in. Every 10 seconds one more letter drops.

    Tap SOLVE when you think you know it. The first buzz wins. Everyone else then has 3 seconds to bet, in secret, on SOLVE or FAIL. No bet in time counts as FAIL. Then the solver has 10 seconds to type the phrase.

    Right answer: the solver and the SOLVE bets are safe, and the FAIL bets take a drink. Wrong answer or out of time: the solver and the SOLVE bets take a drink, and the solver sits out until 2 more letters drop.

    Play responsibly

    Legal drinking age only. One drink per player per round at most, every drink has a no-drink alternative, and passing is always fine. Full rules.
