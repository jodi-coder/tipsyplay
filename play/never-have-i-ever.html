About solo Never Have I Ever

This is the one-phone version of Never Have I Ever. Pick Mild, Spicy, or Extra, tap Next card, and read each prompt out loud. Everyone decides for themselves whether it applies to them. Use the plus and minus buttons to keep points if your group likes a score, and Reshuffle to start the deck again.

Switch on non-alcoholic mode for an alternative to the sip. Sipping is always optional, water counts, and folding a finger or scoring a point is a full way to play. Nothing stacks.

Want everyone answering on their own phone? Host a room from the multiplayer Never Have I Ever page and friends join with a short code. You can also browse more party games for adults, including card drinking games like King's Cup and Card Lottery.

Play responsibly. You must be of legal drinking age in your location. There is no chugging, no races, and no pressure to finish a drink. Plan a ride home before anyone drinks. Read the house code.
