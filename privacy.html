Privacy

This is a short, plain-language note about how this site works. It is not a formal privacy policy written by a lawyer, and it does not claim compliance with any specific law.

On this device

TipsyPlay saves a few settings in your browser's local storage: whether you confirmed your age, favourites, sound on or off, non-alcoholic mode, reminder cards, spice level, party-mode names, and the name you type for an online room. A room code can sit in session storage so a refresh can rejoin. You can clear this data in your browser settings.

Online rooms

If you host or join a room, the site uses Firebase Anonymous Authentication and Firebase Realtime Database so phones in that room can share the game state. The name you type is stored with that anonymous account for the life of the room. Rooms are removed when the host closes them, or after they expire.

TipsyPlay does not ask for an email address or a password to play. We do not sell personal information. We do not run our own ad network on these pages.
