Sparkling Cranberry Punch

A red, bubbly punch that suits the holidays.

⏱ 5 minServes 10Punch bowlWine and bubbly

Ingredients

  • 1 bottle (750 ml) sparkling wine, chilled
  • 1 cup (250 ml) vodka
  • 4 cups (1 L) cranberry juice
  • 2 cups (500 ml) ginger ale
  • 1 cup fresh or frozen cranberries
  • 1 orange, sliced
  • Ice

How to make it

  1. Stir the vodka and cranberry juice in a punch bowl and chill.
  2. Just before serving, add the ginger ale, sparkling wine, cranberries and orange slices.
  3. Add ice and serve.

Make it alcohol-free

Leave out the vodka and use sparkling apple juice in place of the sparkling wine.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
