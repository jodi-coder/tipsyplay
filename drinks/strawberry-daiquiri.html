Strawberry Daiquiri

Rum, lime and frozen strawberries. Bright red and thick enough for a straw.

⏱ 5 minServes 2Hurricane glassRum

Ingredients

  • 4 oz (120 ml) white rum
  • 2 cups frozen strawberries
  • 1½ oz (45 ml) fresh lime juice
  • 1 oz (30 ml) simple syrup
  • 1 cup ice
  • 2 fresh strawberries

How to make it

  1. Blend the rum, frozen strawberries, lime juice, syrup and ice until smooth.
  2. Pour into 2 glasses.
  3. Garnish each with a fresh strawberry.

Make it alcohol-free

Replace the rum with 4 oz orange juice.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
