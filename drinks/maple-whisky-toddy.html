Maple Whisky Toddy

A hot toddy made with Canadian whisky and maple syrup.

⏱ 5 minServes 1MugWhisky

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz (45 ml) Canadian whisky
  • 1 tbsp maple syrup
  • ½ oz (15 ml) fresh lemon juice
  • 6 oz (180 ml) hot water
  • 1 cinnamon stick

How to make it

  1. Stir the whisky, maple syrup and lemon juice in a warm mug.
  2. Top with the hot water and stir.
  3. Add the cinnamon stick.

Make it alcohol-free

Leave out the whisky and use hot black tea in place of the water.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
