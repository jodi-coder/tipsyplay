Hot Buttered Rum

Dark rum with spiced butter and brown sugar melted into hot water.

⏱ 5 minServes 1MugRum

Ingredients

  • 2 oz (60 ml) dark rum
  • 1 tbsp soft butter
  • 2 tsp brown sugar
  • Pinch each of cinnamon and nutmeg
  • 6 oz (180 ml) hot water
  • 1 cinnamon stick

How to make it

  1. Mash the butter, sugar and spices together in a warm mug.
  2. Add the rum and the hot water and stir until the butter melts.
  3. Add the cinnamon stick.

Make it alcohol-free

Use hot apple cider in place of the rum and water.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
