Kamikaze Shot

Equal parts vodka, orange liqueur and lime. Tart, clean and easy to batch.

⏱ 3 minMakes 1 shotShot glassVodka

Ingredients

  • ½ oz (15 ml) vodka
  • ½ oz (15 ml) triple sec
  • ½ oz (15 ml) fresh lime juice

How to make it

  1. Shake everything with ice until the shaker is cold.
  2. Strain into a shot glass.

Make it alcohol-free

Shake 1 oz limeade with a splash of orange juice.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
