French 75

Gin, lemon and sugar topped with sparkling wine.

⏱ 4 minServes 1FluteGin

Ingredients

  • 1 oz (30 ml) gin
  • ½ oz (15 ml) fresh lemon juice
  • ½ oz (15 ml) simple syrup
  • 3 oz (90 ml) sparkling wine, chilled
  • 1 strip of lemon peel

How to make it

  1. Shake the gin, lemon juice and syrup with ice.
  2. Strain into a flute.
  3. Top slowly with the sparkling wine and garnish with the lemon peel.

Make it alcohol-free

Shake the lemon juice and syrup with ice and top with alcohol-free sparkling wine or soda water.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
