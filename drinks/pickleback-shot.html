Pickleback

A shot of whiskey followed by a shot of pickle brine. The brine takes the burn away.

⏱ 2 minMakes 1 shotShot glassWhisky

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz (45 ml) Irish whiskey or bourbon
  • 1½ oz (45 ml) dill pickle brine, chilled

How to make it

  1. Pour the whiskey into one shot glass and the brine into another.
  2. Drink the whiskey, then the brine.

Make it alcohol-free

Skip the whiskey and have the cold brine on its own, or chase ginger ale with it.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
