Glögg

Scandinavian mulled wine with cardamom, served over raisins and almonds.

⏱ 30 minServes 6MugWine and bubbly

Ingredients

  • 1 bottle (750 ml) dry red wine
  • ½ cup (125 ml) vodka
  • ¼ cup sugar
  • 2 cinnamon sticks
  • 6 cardamom pods, cracked
  • 6 whole cloves
  • 2 strips of orange peel
  • Raisins and blanched almonds, for the cups

How to make it

  1. Warm the wine, sugar, spices and orange peel in a pot over low heat for 25 minutes. Do not let it boil.
  2. Take the pot off the heat and stir in the vodka.
  3. Put a few raisins and almonds in each cup and ladle the glögg over them through a sieve.

Make it alcohol-free

Use 3 cups blackcurrant juice and 1 cup water in place of the wine and vodka.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
