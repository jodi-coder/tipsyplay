Manhattan

Rye whisky and sweet vermouth, stirred and served up with a cherry.

⏱ 4 minServes 1CoupeWhisky

Ingredients

  • 2 oz (60 ml) rye whisky
  • 1 oz (30 ml) sweet vermouth
  • 2 dashes aromatic bitters
  • 1 cocktail cherry

How to make it

  1. Stir the whisky, vermouth and bitters with ice for 30 seconds.
  2. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  3. Garnish with the cherry.

Make it alcohol-free

Stir 2 oz cold black tea with 1 oz tart cherry juice over ice and strain.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
