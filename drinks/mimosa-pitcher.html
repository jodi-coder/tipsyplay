Mimosa Pitcher

Sparkling wine and orange juice for a crowd. Build it right before you pour.

⏱ 5 minServes 10PitcherWine and bubbly

Ingredients

  • 2 bottles (750 ml each) sparkling wine, well chilled
  • 3 cups (750 ml) orange juice, well chilled
  • ½ cup (125 ml) orange liqueur
  • 1 orange, sliced

How to make it

  1. Pour the orange juice and orange liqueur into a large pitcher.
  2. Add the sparkling wine slowly, tilting the pitcher so it keeps its bubbles.
  3. Add the orange slices and serve straight away in flutes.

Make it alcohol-free

Use alcohol-free sparkling wine or sparkling white grape juice and leave out the liqueur.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
