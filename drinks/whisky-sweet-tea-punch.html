Whisky Sweet Tea Punch

Bourbon and sweet iced tea with lemon and peaches.

⏱ 10 minServes 10PitcherWhisky

Ingredients

  • 2 cups (500 ml) bourbon
  • 6 cups (1.5 L) sweet iced tea
  • ½ cup (125 ml) fresh lemon juice
  • 2 peaches, sliced
  • 1 handful of fresh mint
  • Ice

How to make it

  1. Stir the bourbon, iced tea and lemon juice in a large pitcher.
  2. Add the peaches and chill for at least 1 hour.
  3. Add the mint and serve over ice.

Make it alcohol-free

Leave out the bourbon. Sweet tea with lemon and peaches is already a good pitcher.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
