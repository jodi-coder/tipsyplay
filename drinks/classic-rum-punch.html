Classic Rum Punch

Two rums, pineapple, orange and lime. The one punch every host should know.

⏱ 10 minServes 12Punch bowlRum

Ingredients

  • 2 cups (500 ml) white rum
  • 1 cup (250 ml) dark rum
  • 3 cups (750 ml) pineapple juice
  • 3 cups (750 ml) orange juice
  • ½ cup (125 ml) fresh lime juice
  • ½ cup (125 ml) grenadine
  • 1 orange, sliced
  • Ice

How to make it

  1. Stir everything except the ice and orange slices in a punch bowl or large pitcher.
  2. Chill for at least 1 hour.
  3. Add the orange slices and plenty of ice just before serving.

Make it alcohol-free

Replace both rums with 3 cups ginger ale, added just before serving.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
