Margarita

Tequila, orange liqueur and fresh lime, with a salted rim.

⏱ 4 minServes 1Rocks glassTequila

Ingredients

  • 2 oz (60 ml) tequila blanco
  • 1 oz (30 ml) orange liqueur
  • 1 oz (30 ml) fresh lime juice
  • Salt, for the rim
  • 1 lime wheel

How to make it

  1. Run a lime wedge around the rim of a rocks glass and dip it in salt. Fill the glass with ice.
  2. Shake the tequila, orange liqueur and lime juice with ice.
  3. Strain into the glass and garnish with the lime wheel.

Make it alcohol-free

Shake 2 oz limeade with 1 oz orange juice and pour it over ice behind the same salt rim.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
