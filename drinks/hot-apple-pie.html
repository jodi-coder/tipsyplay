Hot Apple Pie

Hot cider and cinnamon whisky under whipped cream. It tastes like the filling.

⏱ 5 minServes 1MugWhisky

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz (45 ml) cinnamon whisky
  • 6 oz (180 ml) hot apple cider
  • Whipped cream
  • Pinch of cinnamon

How to make it

  1. Heat the cider until steaming and pour it into a mug.
  2. Stir in the cinnamon whisky.
  3. Top with whipped cream and a pinch of cinnamon.

Make it alcohol-free

Leave out the whisky and stir a pinch of cinnamon into the hot cider.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
