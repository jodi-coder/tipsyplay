PB&J Shot

Peanut butter whisky and raspberry liqueur. It really does taste like the sandwich.

⏱ 2 minMakes 1 shotShot glassWhisky

Ingredients

  • ¾ oz (22 ml) peanut butter whisky
  • ¾ oz (22 ml) raspberry liqueur

How to make it

  1. Shake both with ice.
  2. Strain into a shot glass.

Make it alcohol-free

Shake 1.5 oz grape juice with 1 tsp smooth peanut butter and strain.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
