Blue Lagoon Punch

Electric blue lemonade punch. It looks great on a table.

⏱ 5 minServes 12Punch bowlVodka

Ingredients

  • 2 cups (500 ml) vodka
  • 1 cup (250 ml) blue curaçao
  • 8 cups (2 L) lemonade
  • 2 cups (500 ml) lemon-lime soda
  • 2 lemons, sliced
  • Ice

How to make it

  1. Stir the vodka, blue curaçao and lemonade in a punch bowl and chill.
  2. Add the soda, lemon slices and ice just before serving.

Make it alcohol-free

Use blue raspberry syrup in place of the curaçao and leave out the vodka.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
