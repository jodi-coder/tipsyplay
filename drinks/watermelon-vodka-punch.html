Watermelon Vodka Punch

Fresh watermelon blended into juice, with vodka, lime and mint.

⏱ 15 minServes 10Punch bowlVodka

Ingredients

  • 8 cups cubed seedless watermelon
  • 2 cups (500 ml) vodka
  • ½ cup (125 ml) fresh lime juice
  • ¼ cup (60 ml) simple syrup
  • 2 cups (500 ml) soda water
  • 1 handful of fresh mint
  • Ice

How to make it

  1. Blend the watermelon until smooth and pour it through a sieve into a punch bowl.
  2. Stir in the vodka, lime juice and syrup and chill.
  3. Add the soda water, mint and ice just before serving.

Make it alcohol-free

Leave out the vodka and add 1 more cup of soda water.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
