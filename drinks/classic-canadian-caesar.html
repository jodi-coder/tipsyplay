Classic Canadian Caesar

Canada's cocktail: vodka and clam-tomato juice with hot sauce, Worcestershire and a celery salt rim.

⏱ 5 minServes 1Tall glassVodka

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz (45 ml) vodka
  • 4 oz (120 ml) clam-tomato juice (such as Clamato)
  • 4 dashes Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 dashes hot sauce
  • Celery salt, for the rim
  • Pinch of salt and black pepper
  • 1 lime wedge
  • 1 celery stalk

How to make it

  1. Run the lime wedge around the rim of a tall glass and dip the rim in celery salt.
  2. Fill the glass with ice.
  3. Add the vodka, Worcestershire, hot sauce, salt and pepper, then top with the clam-tomato juice.
  4. Stir well. Garnish with the celery stalk and the lime wedge.

Make it alcohol-free

Leave out the vodka for a Virgin Caesar. Everything else stays the same.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
