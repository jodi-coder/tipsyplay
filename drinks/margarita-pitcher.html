Margarita Pitcher

Margaritas for eight without shaking each one.

⏱ 10 minServes 8PitcherTequila

Ingredients

  • 2 cups (500 ml) tequila blanco
  • 1 cup (250 ml) orange liqueur
  • 1 cup (250 ml) fresh lime juice
  • ½ cup (125 ml) simple syrup
  • 1 cup (250 ml) cold water
  • Salt and lime wedges, for the glasses
  • Ice

How to make it

  1. Stir the tequila, orange liqueur, lime juice, syrup and water in a pitcher.
  2. Chill for at least 1 hour.
  3. Salt the rims of the glasses, fill them with ice and pour.

Make it alcohol-free

Stir 3 cups limeade with 1 cup orange juice and 1 cup soda water.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
