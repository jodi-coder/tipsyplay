Frozen Paloma

Tequila and grapefruit as a pink slush with a salted rim.

⏱ 5 minServes 2Rocks glassTequila

Ingredients

  • 4 oz (120 ml) tequila blanco
  • 4 oz (120 ml) grapefruit juice
  • 1 oz (30 ml) fresh lime juice
  • 1 oz (30 ml) agave syrup
  • 3 cups ice
  • Salt, for the glasses

How to make it

  1. Salt the rims of 2 glasses.
  2. Blend the tequila, grapefruit juice, lime juice, agave syrup and ice until smooth.
  3. Pour into the glasses.

Make it alcohol-free

Replace the tequila with 4 oz more grapefruit juice.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
