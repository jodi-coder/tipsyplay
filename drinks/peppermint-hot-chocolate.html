Peppermint Hot Chocolate

Hot chocolate with peppermint schnapps and crushed candy cane.

⏱ 5 minServes 1MugLiqueur

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz (45 ml) peppermint schnapps
  • 8 oz (240 ml) hot chocolate
  • Whipped cream
  • 1 crushed candy cane

How to make it

  1. Make the hot chocolate and pour it into a mug.
  2. Stir in the peppermint schnapps.
  3. Top with whipped cream and the crushed candy cane.

Make it alcohol-free

Stir in 2 drops of peppermint extract in place of the schnapps.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
