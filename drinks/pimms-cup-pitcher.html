Pimm's Cup Pitcher

The British summer pitcher, loaded with cucumber, strawberries and mint.

⏱ 10 minServes 8PitcherLiqueur

Ingredients

  • 2 cups (500 ml) Pimm's No. 1 (gin-based fruit liqueur)
  • 6 cups (1.5 L) sparkling lemonade or ginger ale
  • 1 cucumber, sliced
  • 1 cup strawberries, sliced
  • 1 orange, sliced
  • 1 handful of fresh mint
  • Ice

How to make it

  1. Half-fill a large pitcher with ice and add the cucumber, strawberries, orange and mint.
  2. Pour in the Pimm's.
  3. Top with the sparkling lemonade, stir gently and serve.

Make it alcohol-free

Use 2 cups cold black tea with a splash of balsamic vinegar in place of the Pimm's.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
