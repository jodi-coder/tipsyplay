Mojito

White rum, lime, mint and soda. Cold, fresh and not too sweet.

⏱ 5 minServes 1Tall glassRum

Ingredients

  • 2 oz (60 ml) white rum
  • ¾ oz (22 ml) fresh lime juice
  • ½ oz (15 ml) simple syrup
  • 8 fresh mint leaves
  • 2 oz (60 ml) soda water
  • 1 mint sprig and 1 lime wheel

How to make it

  1. Press the mint leaves gently with the syrup and lime juice in the bottom of a tall glass. Do not shred them.
  2. Add the rum and fill the glass with ice.
  3. Top with soda water and stir from the bottom.
  4. Garnish with the mint sprig and lime wheel.

Make it alcohol-free

Leave out the rum and add an extra 2 oz soda water for a Virgin Mojito.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
