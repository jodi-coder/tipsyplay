Cinnamon Toast Shot

Cinnamon whisky and rum cream. It tastes like the milk at the bottom of the cereal bowl.

⏱ 3 minMakes 1 shotShot glassWhisky

Ingredients

  • ¾ oz (22 ml) cinnamon whisky
  • ¾ oz (22 ml) rum cream liqueur
  • Cinnamon sugar, for the rim

How to make it

  1. Wet the rim of a shot glass and dip it in cinnamon sugar.
  2. Shake the whisky and rum cream with ice.
  3. Strain into the glass.

Make it alcohol-free

Shake 1.5 oz cold milk with a pinch of cinnamon and a little vanilla.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
