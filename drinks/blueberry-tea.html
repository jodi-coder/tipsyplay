Blueberry Tea

A Canadian favourite with no blueberries in it. Amaretto and orange liqueur in hot tea somehow taste like them.

⏱ 5 minServes 1SnifterLiqueur

Ingredients

  • 1 oz (30 ml) amaretto
  • 1 oz (30 ml) orange liqueur
  • 5 oz (150 ml) hot black tea (orange pekoe is traditional)
  • 1 orange slice

How to make it

  1. Brew the tea and let it steep for 3 minutes.
  2. Pour the amaretto and orange liqueur into a warm snifter or mug.
  3. Top with the hot tea and add the orange slice.

Make it alcohol-free

Stir 2 drops of almond extract and a squeeze of orange into hot black tea.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
