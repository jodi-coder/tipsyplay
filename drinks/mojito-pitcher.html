Mojito Pitcher

A pitcher of mojitos, so nobody is stuck pressing mint all night.

⏱ 10 minServes 8PitcherRum

Ingredients

  • 2 cups (500 ml) white rum
  • 1 cup (250 ml) fresh lime juice
  • ¾ cup (175 ml) simple syrup
  • 1 large bunch of fresh mint (about 40 leaves)
  • 4 cups (1 L) soda water
  • 2 limes, sliced
  • Ice

How to make it

  1. Press the mint gently with the syrup and lime juice in the bottom of a large pitcher.
  2. Add the rum and stir.
  3. Fill the pitcher with ice, add the lime slices and top with the soda water. Stir from the bottom.

Make it alcohol-free

Leave out the rum and add 2 more cups of soda water.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
