Miami Vice

Half strawberry daiquiri, half piña colada, poured side by side in one glass.

⏱ 10 minServes 2Hurricane glassRum

Ingredients

  • 4 oz (120 ml) white rum, divided
  • 1 cup frozen strawberries
  • ½ oz (15 ml) fresh lime juice
  • ½ oz (15 ml) simple syrup
  • 2 oz (60 ml) cream of coconut
  • 2 oz (60 ml) pineapple juice
  • 1 cup frozen pineapple chunks
  • 1 cup ice, divided

How to make it

  1. Blend 2 oz of the rum with the strawberries, lime juice, syrup and half the ice. Pour into a jug and rinse the blender.
  2. Blend the other 2 oz of rum with the cream of coconut, pineapple juice, frozen pineapple and the rest of the ice.
  3. Pour both into each glass at the same time, one from each side, so they sit in two halves.

Make it alcohol-free

Leave out the rum and add 1 oz orange juice to the strawberry half and 1 oz pineapple juice to the coconut half.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
