Mexican Candy Shot

Sweet watermelon with a chili kick, like the lollipop it is named after.

⏱ 4 minMakes 1 shotShot glassTequila

Ingredients

  • 1 oz (30 ml) tequila blanco
  • ½ oz (15 ml) watermelon liqueur
  • 2 dashes hot sauce
  • Chili-lime seasoning, for the rim
  • 1 lime wedge

How to make it

  1. Run the lime wedge around the rim of a shot glass and dip it in chili-lime seasoning.
  2. Shake the tequila, watermelon liqueur and hot sauce with ice.
  3. Strain into the glass.

Make it alcohol-free

Shake 1.5 oz watermelon juice with a squeeze of lime and a dash of hot sauce.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
