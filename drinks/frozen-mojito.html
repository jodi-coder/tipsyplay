Frozen Mojito

All the mint and lime of a mojito, blended into a slush.

⏱ 5 minServes 2Tall glassRum

Ingredients

  • 4 oz (120 ml) white rum
  • 2 oz (60 ml) fresh lime juice
  • 1½ oz (45 ml) simple syrup
  • 12 fresh mint leaves
  • 3 cups ice
  • 2 mint sprigs

How to make it

  1. Blend the rum, lime juice, syrup, mint leaves and ice until smooth and flecked with green.
  2. Pour into 2 glasses.
  3. Garnish with the mint sprigs.

Make it alcohol-free

Replace the rum with 4 oz soda water, stirred in after blending.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
