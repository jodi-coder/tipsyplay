Whiskey Sour

Bourbon balanced with lemon and sugar. A good first cocktail to learn.

⏱ 4 minServes 1Rocks glassWhisky

Ingredients

  • 2 oz (60 ml) bourbon
  • ¾ oz (22 ml) fresh lemon juice
  • ¾ oz (22 ml) simple syrup
  • 1 orange slice and 1 cherry

How to make it

  1. Shake the bourbon, lemon juice and syrup hard with ice.
  2. Strain into a rocks glass filled with fresh ice.
  3. Garnish with the orange slice and cherry.

Make it alcohol-free

Shake 2 oz cold black tea with the lemon juice and syrup.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
