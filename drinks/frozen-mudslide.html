Frozen Mudslide

A boozy milkshake with vodka, coffee liqueur, Irish cream and vanilla ice cream.

⏱ 5 minServes 2Hurricane glassVodka

Ingredients

  • 2 oz (60 ml) vodka
  • 2 oz (60 ml) coffee liqueur
  • 2 oz (60 ml) Irish cream liqueur
  • 2 cups vanilla ice cream
  • 1 cup ice
  • Chocolate syrup, for the glasses

How to make it

  1. Drizzle chocolate syrup around the inside of 2 glasses.
  2. Blend the vodka, coffee liqueur, Irish cream, ice cream and ice until smooth.
  3. Pour into the glasses.

Make it alcohol-free

Blend the ice cream with 4 oz cold coffee and 2 oz milk.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
