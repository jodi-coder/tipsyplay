Tropical Tequila Punch

Tequila with pineapple, mango and lime.

⏱ 10 minServes 10Punch bowlTequila

Ingredients

  • 2 cups (500 ml) tequila blanco
  • 3 cups (750 ml) pineapple juice
  • 2 cups (500 ml) mango nectar
  • ½ cup (125 ml) fresh lime juice
  • 2 cups (500 ml) soda water
  • 1 cup pineapple chunks
  • Ice

How to make it

  1. Stir the tequila, pineapple juice, mango nectar and lime juice in a punch bowl and chill.
  2. Add the soda water, pineapple chunks and ice just before serving.

Make it alcohol-free

Leave out the tequila and add 2 more cups of soda water.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
