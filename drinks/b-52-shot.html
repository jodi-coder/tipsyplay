B-52 Shot

Three neat layers: coffee liqueur, Irish cream and orange liqueur.

⏱ 4 minMakes 1 shotShot glassLiqueur

Ingredients

  • ½ oz (15 ml) coffee liqueur
  • ½ oz (15 ml) Irish cream liqueur
  • ½ oz (15 ml) orange liqueur

How to make it

  1. Pour the coffee liqueur into a shot glass.
  2. Hold a spoon upside down just above it and pour the Irish cream slowly over the back so it floats.
  3. Float the orange liqueur on top the same way.

Make it alcohol-free

Layer cold coffee, then cream, then a little orange juice. It will not hold as sharply, but it tastes good.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
