Apple Cider Bourbon Punch

A fall punch with cider, bourbon and ginger beer.

⏱ 10 minServes 12Punch bowlWhisky

Ingredients

  • 2 cups (500 ml) bourbon
  • 6 cups (1.5 L) apple cider (the cloudy, non-alcoholic kind)
  • ½ cup (125 ml) fresh lemon juice
  • 3 cups (750 ml) ginger beer
  • 2 apples, thinly sliced
  • 3 cinnamon sticks
  • Ice

How to make it

  1. Stir the bourbon, apple cider and lemon juice in a punch bowl and chill.
  2. Add the ginger beer, apple slices and cinnamon sticks just before serving.
  3. Serve over ice.

Make it alcohol-free

Leave out the bourbon and add 1 more cup of ginger beer.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
