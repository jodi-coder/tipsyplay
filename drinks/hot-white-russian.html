Hot White Russian

The cold classic turned into a coffee: vodka, coffee liqueur and cream.

⏱ 3 minServes 1MugVodka

Ingredients

  • 1 oz (30 ml) vodka
  • 1 oz (30 ml) coffee liqueur
  • 5 oz (150 ml) hot coffee
  • 1 oz (30 ml) heavy cream

How to make it

  1. Pour the vodka and coffee liqueur into a warm mug.
  2. Add the hot coffee.
  3. Pour the cream slowly over the back of a spoon so it swirls on top.

Make it alcohol-free

Use hot coffee with 1 tsp sugar, a drop of vanilla and the cream.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
