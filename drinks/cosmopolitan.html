Cosmopolitan

Citrus vodka, orange liqueur, lime and just enough cranberry to turn it pink.

⏱ 4 minServes 1Martini glassVodka

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz (45 ml) citrus vodka
  • ½ oz (15 ml) orange liqueur
  • ½ oz (15 ml) fresh lime juice
  • 1 oz (30 ml) cranberry juice
  • 1 strip of orange peel

How to make it

  1. Shake everything with ice until very cold.
  2. Strain into a chilled martini glass.
  3. Garnish with the orange peel.

Make it alcohol-free

Shake 2 oz cranberry juice with 1 oz orange juice and the lime juice.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
