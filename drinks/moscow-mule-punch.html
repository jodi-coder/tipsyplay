Moscow Mule Punch

Vodka, lime and ginger beer, scaled up for the table.

⏱ 5 minServes 10Punch bowlVodka

Ingredients

  • 2 cups (500 ml) vodka
  • ¾ cup (175 ml) fresh lime juice
  • 6 cups (1.5 L) ginger beer, chilled
  • 2 limes, sliced
  • 1 handful of fresh mint
  • Ice

How to make it

  1. Stir the vodka and lime juice in a punch bowl or pitcher and chill.
  2. Add the ginger beer, lime slices and mint just before serving.
  3. Serve over ice.

Make it alcohol-free

Leave out the vodka. Ginger beer, lime and mint are enough.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
