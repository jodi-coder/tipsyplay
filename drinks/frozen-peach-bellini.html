Frozen Peach Bellini

Frozen peaches and prosecco. A brunch drink that takes five minutes.

⏱ 5 minServes 4FluteWine and bubbly

Ingredients

  • 3 cups frozen peach slices
  • 2 oz (60 ml) peach schnapps
  • 1 oz (30 ml) simple syrup
  • 1 bottle (750 ml) prosecco, chilled

How to make it

  1. Blend the peaches, schnapps, syrup and 2 cups of the prosecco until smooth.
  2. Pour into 4 glasses, filling each about two-thirds.
  3. Top with the rest of the prosecco.

Make it alcohol-free

Use sparkling white grape juice in place of the prosecco and leave out the schnapps.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
