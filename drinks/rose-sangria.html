Rosé Sangria

Rosé with strawberries and raspberries. Pink, cold and easy to drink.

⏱ 10 minServes 12PitcherWine and bubbly

Ingredients

  • 2 bottles (750 ml each) dry rosé
  • ½ cup (125 ml) brandy
  • 2 cups strawberries, sliced
  • 1 cup raspberries
  • 1 lemon, sliced
  • 2 cups (500 ml) lemon-lime soda
  • Ice

How to make it

  1. Stir the rosé, brandy and fruit in a large pitcher.
  2. Chill for at least 2 hours.
  3. Add the soda just before serving and pour over ice.

Make it alcohol-free

Use 6 cups pink lemonade in place of the rosé and brandy.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
