Frozen Margarita

The blender version of the classic: tequila, lime and orange liqueur as a slush.

⏱ 5 minServes 2CoupeTequila

Ingredients

  • 4 oz (120 ml) tequila blanco
  • 2 oz (60 ml) orange liqueur
  • 2 oz (60 ml) fresh lime juice
  • 1 oz (30 ml) simple syrup
  • 3 cups ice
  • Salt and lime wedges, for the glasses

How to make it

  1. Salt the rims of 2 glasses.
  2. Blend the tequila, orange liqueur, lime juice, syrup and ice until smooth.
  3. Pour into the glasses and garnish with lime.

Make it alcohol-free

Blend 6 oz limeade with 2 oz orange juice and the ice.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
