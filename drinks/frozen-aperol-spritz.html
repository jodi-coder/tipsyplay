Frozen Aperol Spritz

The orange spritz, blended with ice for a hot day.

⏱ 5 minServes 2Wine glassWine and bubbly

Ingredients

  • 4 oz (120 ml) orange aperitif (such as Aperol)
  • 6 oz (180 ml) prosecco, chilled
  • 2 oz (60 ml) orange juice
  • 3 cups ice
  • 2 orange slices

How to make it

  1. Blend the aperitif, prosecco, orange juice and ice until smooth.
  2. Pour into 2 large wine glasses.
  3. Garnish with the orange slices.

Make it alcohol-free

Blend an alcohol-free orange aperitif with orange juice, soda water and the ice.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
