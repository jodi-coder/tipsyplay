Piña Colada

Rum, pineapple and cream of coconut, blended thick.

⏱ 5 minServes 2Hurricane glassRum

Ingredients

  • 4 oz (120 ml) white rum
  • 4 oz (120 ml) cream of coconut
  • 4 oz (120 ml) pineapple juice
  • 1½ cups frozen pineapple chunks
  • 1 cup ice
  • 2 pineapple wedges and 2 cherries

How to make it

  1. Blend the rum, cream of coconut, pineapple juice, frozen pineapple and ice until smooth.
  2. Pour into 2 glasses.
  3. Garnish with the pineapple wedges and cherries.

Make it alcohol-free

Leave out the rum and add 2 oz more pineapple juice for a Virgin Colada.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
