Espresso Martini

Vodka, coffee liqueur and fresh espresso, shaken until it has a thick foam.

⏱ 5 minServes 1Martini glassVodka

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz (45 ml) vodka
  • 1 oz (30 ml) coffee liqueur
  • 1 oz (30 ml) fresh espresso, cooled slightly
  • ¼ oz (7 ml) simple syrup
  • 3 coffee beans

How to make it

  1. Shake everything very hard with plenty of ice for 15 seconds. The hard shake makes the foam.
  2. Strain into a chilled martini glass.
  3. Float the coffee beans on the foam.

Make it alcohol-free

Shake 2 oz espresso with 0.5 oz simple syrup and a drop of vanilla.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
