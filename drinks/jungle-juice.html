Jungle Juice

The big-batch party punch. This version is fruity and balanced, not a dare.

⏱ 15 minServes 24Punch bowlVodka

Ingredients

  • 2 cups (500 ml) vodka
  • 1 cup (250 ml) white rum
  • ½ cup (125 ml) triple sec
  • 4 cups (1 L) fruit punch
  • 4 cups (1 L) orange juice
  • 4 cups (1 L) pineapple juice
  • 4 cups (1 L) lemonade
  • 2 oranges, 2 lemons and 2 cups strawberries, sliced
  • Ice

How to make it

  1. Stir all the liquids together in a very large bowl or a clean drinks cooler.
  2. Add the sliced fruit and chill for at least 2 hours.
  3. Add ice just before serving and stir again.

Make it alcohol-free

Leave out the vodka, rum and triple sec and add 3 cups lemon-lime soda before serving.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
