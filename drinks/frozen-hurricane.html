Frozen Hurricane

Two rums with passion fruit and orange, blended and served in the glass named after it.

⏱ 5 minServes 2Hurricane glassRum

Ingredients

  • 2 oz (60 ml) white rum
  • 2 oz (60 ml) dark rum
  • 4 oz (120 ml) passion fruit juice
  • 2 oz (60 ml) orange juice
  • 1 oz (30 ml) fresh lime juice
  • ½ oz (15 ml) grenadine
  • 3 cups ice
  • 2 orange slices and 2 cherries

How to make it

  1. Blend everything except the garnish until smooth.
  2. Pour into 2 glasses.
  3. Garnish with the orange slices and cherries.

Make it alcohol-free

Replace both rums with 4 oz more orange juice.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
