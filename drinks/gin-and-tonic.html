Gin and Tonic

Gin, cold tonic, lots of ice and a wedge of lime.

⏱ 2 minServes 1Tall glassGin

Ingredients

  • 2 oz (60 ml) gin
  • 4 oz (120 ml) tonic water, chilled
  • 1 lime wedge

How to make it

  1. Fill a tall glass to the top with ice.
  2. Add the gin, then pour in the tonic slowly.
  3. Squeeze in the lime wedge, drop it in and stir once.

Make it alcohol-free

Tonic water with lime and a few crushed juniper berries or a cucumber slice.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
