Paloma Pitcher

Tequila, grapefruit and lime, topped with soda at the last minute.

⏱ 10 minServes 8PitcherTequila

Ingredients

  • 2 cups (500 ml) tequila blanco
  • 2 cups (500 ml) grapefruit juice
  • ½ cup (125 ml) fresh lime juice
  • ¼ cup (60 ml) agave syrup
  • 3 cups (750 ml) soda water
  • 1 grapefruit, sliced
  • Ice

How to make it

  1. Stir the tequila, grapefruit juice, lime juice and agave syrup in a pitcher until the syrup dissolves.
  2. Chill for at least 1 hour.
  3. Add the soda water and grapefruit slices just before serving and pour over ice.

Make it alcohol-free

Leave out the tequila and add 1 more cup of grapefruit juice.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
