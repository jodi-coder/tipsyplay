Paloma

Tequila and grapefruit soda with lime and a pinch of salt.

⏱ 3 minServes 1Tall glassTequila

Ingredients

  • 2 oz (60 ml) tequila blanco
  • ½ oz (15 ml) fresh lime juice
  • 4 oz (120 ml) grapefruit soda
  • Pinch of salt
  • 1 grapefruit wedge

How to make it

  1. Fill a tall glass with ice.
  2. Add the tequila, lime juice and salt.
  3. Top with grapefruit soda, stir once and garnish with the grapefruit wedge.

Make it alcohol-free

Leave out the tequila. Grapefruit soda with lime and salt works well alone.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
