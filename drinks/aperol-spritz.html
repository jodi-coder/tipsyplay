Aperol Spritz

Three parts prosecco, two parts orange aperitif, one part soda.

⏱ 2 minServes 1Wine glassWine and bubbly

Ingredients

  • 3 oz (90 ml) prosecco
  • 2 oz (60 ml) orange aperitif (such as Aperol)
  • 1 oz (30 ml) soda water
  • 1 orange slice

How to make it

  1. Fill a large wine glass with ice.
  2. Add the prosecco, then the aperitif, then the soda.
  3. Stir once and add the orange slice.

Make it alcohol-free

Use an alcohol-free orange aperitif with soda water and a splash of orange juice.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
