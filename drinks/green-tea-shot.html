Green Tea Shot

No tea in it. Whiskey, peach and sour mix that happen to come out pale green.

⏱ 3 minMakes 2 shotsShot glassWhisky

Ingredients

  • 1 oz (30 ml) Irish whiskey
  • 1 oz (30 ml) peach schnapps
  • 1 oz (30 ml) sour mix
  • Splash of lemon-lime soda

How to make it

  1. Shake the whiskey, schnapps and sour mix with ice.
  2. Strain into 2 shot glasses.
  3. Top each with a splash of soda.

Make it alcohol-free

Shake 2 oz peach iced tea with a squeeze of lemon and top with soda.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
