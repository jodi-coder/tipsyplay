Long Island Iced Tea

Five spirits, lemon and a splash of cola. It is stronger than it tastes, so one is plenty.

⏱ 5 minServes 1Tall glassVodka

Ingredients

  • ½ oz (15 ml) vodka
  • ½ oz (15 ml) gin
  • ½ oz (15 ml) white rum
  • ½ oz (15 ml) tequila blanco
  • ½ oz (15 ml) triple sec
  • ¾ oz (22 ml) fresh lemon juice
  • ½ oz (15 ml) simple syrup
  • 2 oz (60 ml) cola
  • 1 lemon wedge

How to make it

  1. Shake everything except the cola with ice.
  2. Strain into a tall glass filled with fresh ice.
  3. Top with the cola, stir once and garnish with the lemon wedge.

Make it alcohol-free

Mix 4 oz cold black tea with the lemon juice and syrup and top with cola.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
