Mini Beer Shot

A golden vanilla liqueur with a cream head, served so it looks like a tiny beer.

⏱ 3 minMakes 1 shotShot glassLiqueur

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz (45 ml) vanilla-citrus liqueur (such as Licor 43), chilled
  • ¼ oz (7 ml) heavy cream

How to make it

  1. Pour the chilled liqueur into a shot glass, leaving a little room.
  2. Float the cream on top over the back of a spoon.

Make it alcohol-free

Use cold apple juice with a spoonful of cream on top.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
