Tom Collins

A tall gin lemonade with bubbles.

⏱ 3 minServes 1Tall glassGin

Ingredients

  • 2 oz (60 ml) gin
  • 1 oz (30 ml) fresh lemon juice
  • ½ oz (15 ml) simple syrup
  • 3 oz (90 ml) soda water
  • 1 lemon wheel and 1 cherry

How to make it

  1. Shake the gin, lemon juice and syrup with ice.
  2. Strain into a tall glass filled with fresh ice.
  3. Top with soda water and garnish with the lemon wheel and cherry.

Make it alcohol-free

Leave out the gin for a sparkling lemonade.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
