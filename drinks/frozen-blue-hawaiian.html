Frozen Blue Hawaiian

A blue piña colada: rum, blue curaçao, coconut and pineapple.

⏱ 5 minServes 2Hurricane glassRum

Ingredients

  • 3 oz (90 ml) white rum
  • 2 oz (60 ml) blue curaçao
  • 2 oz (60 ml) cream of coconut
  • 4 oz (120 ml) pineapple juice
  • 3 cups ice
  • 2 pineapple wedges

How to make it

  1. Blend everything except the pineapple wedges until smooth.
  2. Pour into 2 glasses.
  3. Garnish with the pineapple wedges.

Make it alcohol-free

Use blue raspberry syrup in place of the curaçao and leave out the rum.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
