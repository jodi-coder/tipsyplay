Melon Ball Shot

Bright green and fruity: melon liqueur, vodka and pineapple.

⏱ 3 minMakes 1 shotShot glassVodka

Ingredients

  • ½ oz (15 ml) melon liqueur
  • ½ oz (15 ml) vodka
  • ½ oz (15 ml) pineapple juice

How to make it

  1. Shake everything with ice.
  2. Strain into a shot glass.

Make it alcohol-free

Blend a few cubes of honeydew with pineapple juice and strain.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
