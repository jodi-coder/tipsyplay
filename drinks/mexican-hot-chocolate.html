Mexican Hot Chocolate

Hot chocolate with cinnamon, a pinch of cayenne and aged tequila.

⏱ 5 minServes 1MugTequila

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz (45 ml) reposado tequila
  • 8 oz (240 ml) hot chocolate
  • Pinch of cinnamon
  • Small pinch of cayenne pepper
  • 1 cinnamon stick

How to make it

  1. Make the hot chocolate and whisk in the cinnamon and cayenne.
  2. Pour into a mug and stir in the tequila.
  3. Add the cinnamon stick.

Make it alcohol-free

Leave out the tequila. The spiced hot chocolate stands on its own.

Good with these games

Pour it, then start a room. Drinking is optional in every game.
