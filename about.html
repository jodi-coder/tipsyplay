About TipsyPlay

TipsyPlay is a free website of party games for adults. You open it on your phone. A host starts a room, and friends join with a code.

Never Have I Ever is the game you can play today. The other games listed on the site are marked coming soon. We add games when they are actually ready, not before.

Every game is opt-in. Every game works with non-alcoholic drinks. Sips, when a game offers them, are one small sip at most. There is no chugging and no penalty that gets bigger.

You must be of legal drinking age in your location.
