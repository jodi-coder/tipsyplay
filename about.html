About TipsyPlay

TipsyPlay is a free website of party games for adults. You open it on your phone. A host starts a room, and friends join with a code.

The party games on the list are ready to play, including Never Have I Ever. We add a game when it is actually ready, not before.

Every game is opt-in. Every game works with non-alcoholic drinks. Drinks, when a game offers them, are one small drink at most. There is no chugging and no penalty that gets bigger.

You must be of legal drinking age in your location.
