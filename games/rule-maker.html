What is Rule Maker?

Rule Maker is a card drinking game for adults where cards invent silly table rules. Slip once and it is one optional sip or a point — then the night continues. There is no escalation ladder and no king's cup mechanic. TipsyPlay keeps the active rules visible so newcomers can catch up.

Designed for 4–10 players who like light structure and a lot of laughter. Host on a phone, friends join with a code, and the deck drives the pace.

Setup

  1. Host a room and share the join code.
  2. Read each new rule aloud when it appears.
  3. Agree that rules stay kind and reversible if someone is uncomfortable.

Rules

  1. Draw or reveal the next card when the app says it is time.
  2. Follow the table rule it creates.
  3. A slip costs one small optional sip — or a point. Nothing stacks.
  4. No pirate-style escalation. One consequence per slip, max.

Variations

  • Soft mode: points only for the first half of the deck.
  • Veto token: each player may retire one rule per night.
  • Theme rules: only gesture rules, or only speech rules.

Because Rule Maker never escalates into a king's cup, it is a safer on-ramp for friends who are new to card drinking games. Keep the active-rules list visible, retire anything awkward, and celebrate creative slips with points when the table prefers a dry night.

Non-alcoholic option

Slip-ups cost a point. Water welcome. Legal drinking age only — play safe.

FAQ

Can I play without drinking?

Yes. A slip can cost a point instead of a sip. Water is welcome. You may decline both a drink and any alternative, with no replacement. Pause or stop instead of recording a refused action.

Does this become a King's Cup?

No. There is no escalation ladder and no king's cup mechanic. One consequence per slip, maximum. Nothing stacks.

How many people can play?

4–10 players.

What if a rule feels unkind?

Agree up front that rules stay kind and reversible. Retire anything awkward — no debate required under the house code.
