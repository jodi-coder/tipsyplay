Vault Traitor

Crack a four-digit code together while one or two hidden Traitors bend the clues. Debate, then vote in secret.

3–8 playersNewNo props
Non-alcoholic alt: Skip your next vote or give up your pass token.
Host

Never Have I Ever

Each phone joins with a code. One small drink or a 3-second pose. Nothing stacks. Includes a Most likely mode where everyone votes for a friend.

2–12 playersEasyNo props
Non-alcoholic alt: Hold a 3-second pose.
Host

Story Chain

Build a ridiculous story one sentence at a time.

3–10 playersEasyNo props
Non-alcoholic alt: Do a 5-second dramatic pose instead.
Host

Hot Take Court

Defend a silly opinion. The group votes guilty or not guilty.

3–8 playersMediumNo props
Non-alcoholic alt: Guilty means you lose a point.
Host

Card Lottery

Flip a card and guess red or black. One optional drink per round, max.

2–8 playersEasyCards
Non-alcoholic alt: Correct guesses score points. Pass skips the color guess.
Host

Category Countdown

Name things in a category before the timer ends.

3–10 playersEasyNo props
Non-alcoholic alt: A stumble means your best animal impression.
Host

Two Truths & a Twist

Share two truths and one fib. The group guesses the fib.

3–10 playersEasyNo props
Non-alcoholic alt: Fooled guessers give you a point.
Host

Sketchy Guess

Draw a secret word on your phone. The sketch shows up for everyone else. Points only: most points wins.

4–12 playersMediumPhones
Non-alcoholic alt: Points only. A correct guess scores 1 for the guesser and 1 for the artist.
Host

Rule Maker

Cards create silly table rules. One optional drink per slip — no escalation, no king's cup.

4–10 playersMediumCards
Non-alcoholic alt: Slip-ups cost a point.
Host

Split Decision

Lock A or B, then predict how many friends picked A. Keeps score in points. Nothing stacks.

2–10 playersEasyNo props
Non-alcoholic alt: Skip the drink: whoever picked the minority side takes a bow.
Host

King's Cup

Each phone joins with a code. Every card is one small drink or a non-alcoholic alternative. Nothing stacks.

2–10 playersMediumCards
Non-alcoholic alt: A pose, a dare, or a point. The cup only counts kings.
