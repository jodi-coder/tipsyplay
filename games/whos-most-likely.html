    How Who's Most Likely works on TipsyPlay

    Who's Most Likely is a voting party game for adults. One person hosts a room and shares a join link. Friends open it, type a name, and land in the same game. The host picks Classic, Spicy, or Couples, plus a length such as 10 rounds.

    Each round puts one original prompt on every phone. A short countdown is only for voting. Tap one person. Votes stay hidden. When everyone still connected has voted, or the answer time runs out, the phones show Sip, water, or pose and the name of the person with the most votes. A tie names everyone tied. Each of them gets one small sip, water, or a pose. Nothing stacks, and there is no timer on the sip.

    A missed vote is not a vote for anyone. If someone disconnects, the table does not wait on that phone. You can add your own question for a later round. The pack keeps going if you don't.

    Non-alcoholic option

    Skip the sip. Water counts, and striking a pose counts. Passing is always fine.

    Play responsibly

    Legal drinking age only. Sips are optional and small. No chugging and no pressure. See the house code. Looking for another game? Open Never Have I Ever or the full games list.
