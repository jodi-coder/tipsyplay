How Who's Most Likely works on TipsyPlay

Who's Most Likely (also called Most Likely To) is a voting party game for adults. TipsyPlay runs it in party mode: join with a PIN, see the prompt on every phone, and point without narrating your vote out loud until the reveal.

After votes land, the group sees who the table picked. That person can take one small optional sip or a playful challenge such as a bow, a pose, or a tiny speech. Nothing stacks. The next prompt starts clean so the night stays light.

This page is the live room for Who's Most Likely. Host from your phone, share the PIN, and keep the mixer handy if you want music and sound effects under control. Packs such as classic stay selectable from the host bar so the table can match the vibe.

Non-alcoholic option

Skip the sip and take the challenge instead — a bow, a pose, or a short speech. Points and dares are a full way to play. Soft drinks and water always count.

Play responsibly

Of legal drinking age only. Optional small sips, no races, no pressure. Read the house code. More voting energy lives next door in Hot Take Court, or browse all party games.
