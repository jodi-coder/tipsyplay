👉 Who's Most Likely

👥 4–12🧰 None😌 easy📱 phone
Non-alcoholic alternative (always fine): Most votes gives a 10-second acceptance speech.
Host Game

You must be of legal drinking age in your location.

Setup

  1. Host a room and share the code. Four or more is ideal. Two can still play.
  2. Agree on a spice of honesty everyone is fine with. Keep it kind.
  3. Non-alcoholic mode swaps the sip for a speech the whole night.

Rules

  1. A prompt shows on every phone.
  2. Each player taps a name in secret.
  3. The person with the most votes may take one small sip, or give a 10-second speech.
  4. Ties sip nothing. Passing is always fine. No one has to explain a vote.

Variations

  • Speech night: turn non-alcoholic mode on and skip sipping entirely.
  • Kind round: only vote for compliments, like who is most likely to share fries.
  • The winner of the vote picks the next person's nickname for one round.
