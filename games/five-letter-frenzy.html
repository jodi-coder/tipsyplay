      How Five Letter Frenzy works
      

      Five Letter Frenzy is a word-guessing game for adults. Find the secret five-letter word in six tries. Any five letters count as a guess, but the secret word is always a real word.
      

      After each guess every tile is marked: ● means the right letter in the right place, ↗ means the letter is in the word but somewhere else, and × means the letter is not in the word.
      

      Three ways to play
        

      • Solo: one player and one board. Play the daily word or a random one, keep a streak and share your result as coloured squares. Solo works offline.
        • 

      • Race: 2 to 8 players, each on their own phone and board, chase the same secret word. Fewest guesses wins, and the fastest finish breaks a tie.
        • 

      • Table: 3 to 8 players share one grid and take turns guessing. A miss is one small optional drink, a 5-second pose, or Pass.
        • 

      

      Non-alcoholic option
      

      Solo and Race are score-only. In Table mode, a 5-second pose or a pass is always a full answer. Legal age only — play responsibly.
