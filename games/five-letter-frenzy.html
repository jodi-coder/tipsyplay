    How to play

      

    1. The host picks one secret five-letter word. Everyone shares one grid.
      2. 

    2. On your turn, type any five letters and tap Guess. They don't have to be a real word, but the secret word always is.
      3. 

    3. Each tile tells you about the secret word: ● right letter, right place. ↗ right letter, wrong place. × not in the word.
      4. 

    4. Guess the whole word to win. The table gets six guesses in total.
      5. 

    5. A miss is one small drink, a 5-second pose, or Pass. Passing is always fine, and nothing stacks.
      6. 

    

    

    Play responsibly: passing is always fine. Nothing stacks, and there is no timer on a drink.
