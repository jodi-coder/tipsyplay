Category Countdown

A category, a short timer, and one player naming things out loud.

You must be of legal drinking age in your location.

Host Game

What is Category Countdown?

Category Countdown is a fast naming game with two modes. In Spotlight, one player races a ring timer to name as many answers as they can while everyone else watches the tiles pop up and challenges the dodgy ones. In Hot Potato, the whole table shares a category and goes round adding one answer each, with less time every lap, until one player is left.

Best with 3–10 players, each on their own phone. A TV or laptop can show the board.

Setup

  1. Host a room. Friends join with the code or the QR.
  2. Optional: open "Show on a TV or laptop" for the big board. Without one, the host's phone shows it.
  3. Pick a mode, a pack (Mild, Spicy or Absurd), a difficulty and a length. Turn on Points only for no drinking.

Spotlight

  1. The active player gets a category and a target ("Fruits: name 7"), after a 3-2-1 count-in.
  2. They have 30 seconds to type answers. Each one pops onto the board, and repeats and near-repeats (plurals, spacing, capitals) are dropped automatically.
  3. Anyone else can tap an answer to challenge it. When time is up, the table gives each challenged answer a thumbs up or down. More downs throws it out, and a tie keeps it.
  4. Hit the target and everyone else takes a drink or does the alternative. Miss and the active player does.

Scoring

1 point per accepted answer, plus 1 for hitting the target.

Hot Potato

  1. Everyone gets the same category. Take turns adding one answer each: 8 seconds on the first lap, 6 on the second, then 5.
  2. After each answer the table has 3 seconds to challenge it, and a challenged answer goes to a quick vote.
  3. A repeat, an answer voted out, or running out of time knocks you out. Take a drink or do the alternative.
  4. The last player standing gets 3 points, and second place gets 1.

Play responsibly

One drink per round at most, every drink has a no-drink option, and passing never costs anything. Of legal drinking age only. House code.
