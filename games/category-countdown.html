What is Category Countdown?

Category Countdown is a quick party game for adults. The app gives a category and a short timer. One player names as many fitting things as they can out loud before the beep. It needs no props beyond phones and works well for 3–10 people who want energy without a long rules lecture.

Host a room, share the code, and rotate who faces the timer. Stumbles are part of the fun — keep the tone kind.

Setup

  1. Host and join with the room code.
  2. Decide whether repeats from earlier rounds are allowed.
  3. Keep phones loud enough to hear the timer.

Rules

  1. When the category appears, start naming aloud.
  2. Stop when the timer ends.
  3. If you freeze or break a house rule, take one small optional sip — or the alternative.
  4. Nothing stacks into the next category.

Variations

  • Team buzz: two players alternate names in one timer.
  • No proper nouns night.
  • Speed round with a shorter timer for the finale.

Why it works as a party game for adults

Category Countdown keeps energy high without props or complicated scoring. Because every stumble has a built-in non-alcoholic alternative, mixed groups stay included. Use it between heavier card drinking games, or as a warm-up before King's Cup. Hosts can restart rooms quickly if the table wants a fresher category pack.

Keep rounds short, celebrate creative answers, and retire any category that makes someone uncomfortable. That is the TipsyPlay standard for games for adults: optional, small, and kind.
