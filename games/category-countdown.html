⏱️ Category Countdown

👥 3–10🧰 None😌 easy📱 phone
Non-alcoholic alternative (always fine): A stumble means your best animal impression.
Host Game

You must be of legal drinking age in your location.

Setup

  1. Host a room. Everyone else joins on their own phone.
  2. The app rotates the namer and shows a 20-second timer.
  3. Non-alcoholic mode keeps the impression and drops the sip.

Rules

  1. The category shows on every phone. One player names things out loud.
  2. They tap +1 for each real answer. No repeats in the same turn.
  3. If they stumble, they may take one small sip or do an animal impression.
  4. The timer ending the turn is the whole consequence. No speed-drinking.

Variations

  • No repeats from earlier rounds. The table enforces it.
  • Team relay: two namers, one shared count.
  • Letter lock: every answer has to start with the same letter.
