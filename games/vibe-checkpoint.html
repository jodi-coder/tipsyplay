Rules

One friend is in the spotlight and rates the idea from 1 to 5. Everyone else guesses that number. Exact is 2 points, next door is 1. The spotlight does not guess or score. The spotlight can pass and hand the turn on; guessers can pass for zero. One full cycle, or two if the host says so. A tie shares the win. If someone leaves, the board shows guesses played.

How Vibe Checkpoint works

Vibe Checkpoint is a phone party game for adults. One person hosts. Friends scan a QR code or type the four-letter room code. A funny hypothetical lands. The spotlight privately picks 1 through 5. Everyone else tries to match that feeling. The scale always runs from least into it to most into it.

Passing is always fine and needs no story. The spotlight can pass and hand the turn on. Guessers can pass for zero. Nothing stacks. Points are the default. A small sip, a wave, or nothing are all fine after the reveal, and both the drink and the wave can be skipped.

Legal drinking age only. See the house code, or try Split Decision and Chaos Crew.

FAQ

Can I play without drinking?

Yes. The game keeps score in points. After a reveal, a small sip, a wave, or nothing are all fine, and both the drink and the wave can be skipped. You may decline both a drink and any alternative, with no replacement.

Is it “points only, pass any time”?

It keeps score in points, but an optional sip/wave/nothing line can still appear after the reveal. Pass is available for the pick — not as a drink-decline button, and not necessarily in every moment.

What if I am in the spotlight and want to skip?

The spotlight can pass and hand the turn on, and guessers can pass for zero. That is a turn control. House policy still covers drinks and alternatives.

How many people can play?

2–10 players. No props; join with a code or QR.
