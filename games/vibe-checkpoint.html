How Vibe Checkpoint works

Vibe Checkpoint is a phone party game for adults. One person hosts. Friends scan a QR code or type the four-letter room code. A funny hypothetical lands. The spotlight privately picks 1 through 5. Everyone else tries to match that feeling. The scale always runs from least into it to most into it.

Passing is always fine and needs no story. The spotlight can pass and hand the turn on. Guessers can pass for zero. Nothing stacks. Points are the default. A small sip, a wave, or nothing are all fine after the reveal, and both the drink and the wave can be skipped.

Legal drinking age only. See the house code, or try Split Decision and Chaos Crew.
