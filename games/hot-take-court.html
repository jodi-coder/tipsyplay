⚖️ Hot Take Court

👥 3–8🧰 None🎯 medium📱 phone
Non-alcoholic alternative (always fine): Guilty means you lose a point.
Host Game

You must be of legal drinking age in your location.

Setup

  1. Host a room. Players join with the code.
  2. The app rotates who is on the stand.
  3. Pick non-alcoholic mode if a guilty verdict should only cost a point.

Rules

  1. The opinion shows on every phone. The player on the stand may defend it in one breath.
  2. Everyone else votes guilty or not guilty.
  3. Guilty: one small sip, or lose a point. Not guilty: the opinion survives and the defender scores.
  4. One consequence per round. No extra penalties, no speed rounds.

Variations

  • Ten-second defense, then the vote locks.
  • Co-counsel: the defender picks one friend who must argue with them.
  • Silent court: no defense, just the vote.
