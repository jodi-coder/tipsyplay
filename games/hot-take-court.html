Party game · 3 to 8 players

Hot Take Court

One player is on the stand. Everyone else votes guilty or not guilty.

You must be of legal drinking age in your location.

Host Game

What is Hot Take Court?

Hot Take Court puts one player on the stand with a hot take to defend. A Prosecutor argues against it, the jury votes in secret, and the verdict flips over one vote at a time before the gavel falls. Everyone plays on their own phone, and a TV or laptop can show the courtroom for the whole room.

Best with 3–8 players. No props: just phones, and a TV if you have one.

Setup

  1. Host a room. Friends join with the code or the QR.
  2. Optional: open "Show on a TV or laptop" and put the display link on a big screen. Without one, the host's phone shows the courtroom.
  3. Pick a pack (Mild, Spicy or Absurd), how many cases, and Points only if the table wants no drinking.

How a case works

  1. The case (10 s). The next player is the Defendant. Their phone privately offers three hot takes; they pick one, or one is picked for them.
  2. Opening argument (30 s). The Defendant argues for it, out loud.
  3. Prosecution (20 s). A random other player argues against it. Any juror can shout "Objection!" once from their phone. It's just for fun and doesn't stop the clock.
  4. Deliberation (10 s). Every juror votes Guilty or Not guilty in secret.
  5. Verdict. The votes flip over one at a time, then GUILTY, NOT GUILTY, or HUNG JURY on a tie, when nobody drinks.
  6. Sentencing. Guilty: the Defendant takes a drink, takes the jury's funny sentence instead, or passes. Not guilty: the Prosecutor takes a drink, does a quick pose, or passes.

Scoring

Not guilty: the Defendant gets 2 points. Guilty: the Prosecutor gets 1. Every juror who voted with the majority gets 1. At the end come the awards: Most Convicted, Smooth Talker, Hanging Judge and Swing Vote.

Play responsibly

One drink per case at most, every drink has a no-drink option, and passing never costs anything. Of legal drinking age only. House code.
