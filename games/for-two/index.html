Games for 2

Two people are a complete game night. Every title here starts at 2 players — from a red-or-black flip to a cup-toss, a would-you-rather style lock, or a classic card circle.

Host on the phones you already have. Keep any sip small and optional. Water, a soft drink, or no drink is a full way to play. You may decline both a drink and any alternative, with no replacement action.

You must be of legal drinking age in your location.
