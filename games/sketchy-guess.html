✏️ Sketchy Guess

👥 4–12✏️ Phone🎯 medium🖼️ draw
Non-alcoholic alternative (always fine): The side with the most correct guesses wins.
Host Game

You must be of legal drinking age in your location.

Setup

  1. Host a room. Players join with the code. Paper is optional. The canvas is on the phone.
  2. Players alternate teams by join order: Yellow, Pink, Yellow, Pink.
  3. The drawer sees the word. Everyone else sees the lines, not the word.

Rules

  1. The drawer has about 45 seconds and a shared canvas. Strokes sync to the other phones.
  2. No letters or numbers in the drawing.
  3. Guessers type on their own phone. A correct guess scores a point for them and for the drawer.
  4. Points only. Nobody sips for a missed sketch.

Variations

  • Team night: only one team guesses while the other draws.
  • One-color challenge: the drawer cannot clear the canvas.
  • After the round, the drawer may explain the masterpiece.
