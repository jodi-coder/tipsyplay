What is Sketchy Guess?

Sketchy Guess is a drawing party game for adults. One player gets a secret word and sketches it on their phone. The drawing appears for everyone else so the room can guess. Scoring is points-only — perfect when you want laughs without any drink ladder.

It fits 4–12 players and needs phones, not paper. Host a room, share the code, and rotate artists each round.

Setup

  1. Host and join so every phone shares the same sketch stream.
  2. Decide team vs free-for-all guessing.
  3. Keep fingers clean enough to draw — snacks after the round.

Rules

  1. The artist draws the secret word. No letters or numbers in the sketch.
  2. Guessers type or call out answers as your table prefers.
  3. Correct guesses score points for the guesser and usually the artist.
  4. The team or player with the most correct guesses wins.

Variations

  • Relay art: two artists swap halfway through the timer.
  • Emoji-only guesses in the chat.
  • Movie night pack: titles and characters only.

Sketchy Guess is already a complete non-drinking party game, which makes it perfect between card drinking games when the table needs a palate cleanser. Encourage wild guesses, ban spoilers of the secret word, and let the high score pick the next TipsyPlay room.

Non-alcoholic option

The team with the most correct guesses wins — already a full non-drinking game. If others sip separately, keep it optional and small. House code.

FAQ

Can I play without drinking?

Yes. The live page says scoring is points-only and the side with the most correct guesses wins. It is already a complete non-drinking party game. Alcohol is never required.

Do we need paper and pens?

No. You draw on the phone, and the sketch appears for everyone else.

How many people can play?

The live listing is 4–12 players — not a two-person game.

Is there a drink penalty for a bad drawing?

No drink ladder is described. Correct guesses score points for the guesser and usually the artist.
