What is Odd Word Out?

Odd Word Out is TipsyPlay's secret-word bluffing game. Everyone sees the same word except one player, the mole, who only sees the category. Each player says a one-word clue out loud, then the group votes for who they think is bluffing. Catch the mole and the group scores. Slip away and the mole scores. It works for 4–10 players with no props.

Every phone gets a private card, so nobody can peek. Host a room, pass the code or QR, and play a few rounds. Drinks are optional, and a no-drink setting is always one tap away.
