Card drinking games for adults

Card drinking games are still the easiest way to turn a table into a party — as long as every sip stays optional, small, and unstacked. On TipsyPlay the phone can be the dealer, so you do not need a sticky deck to start.

These card games for adults run in the browser. Host a room, share a code, and play King’s Cup, Card Lottery, or Rule Maker. Water and soft drinks count. You may decline both a drink and any alternative, and you do not owe a replacement action.

Read the house code before the first card, and keep food and water on the table.

You must be of legal drinking age in your location.
