No-props party games for adults

No deck, no cups, no scavenger hunt for pens. These no-props party games for adults mean phones only — the ones already in your pockets.

Host a room, share a code, and the table is ready: a never-have-I-ever, a shared story, a most-likely-to vote, a silly debate, a timed naming burst, or a would-you-rather style lock. Alcohol is never required. Sips, if anyone chooses them, stay optional and small. You may decline both a drink and any alternative, and you do not owe a replacement action. If a screen only offers a choice someone has refused, pause or stop rather than recording it.

Water and soft drinks count. Read the house code, then play.

You must be of legal drinking age in your location.
