No-props party games for adults

No deck, no cups, no scavenger hunt for pens. These no-props party games for adults mean phones only — the ones already in your pockets.

Host a room, share a code, and the table is ready: a never-have-I-ever, a shared story, a most-likely-to vote, a silly debate, a timed naming burst, or a would-you-rather style lock. Alcohol is never required. Drinks, if anyone chooses them, stay optional and small. You may decline both a drink and any alternative, and you do not owe a replacement action. If a screen only offers a choice someone has refused, pause or stop rather than recording it.

Soft drinks count. Read the house code, then play.

You must be of legal drinking age in your location.
