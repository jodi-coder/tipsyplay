Quick party games for adults

Sometimes the table wants a game that starts in a minute, not a rulebook. These quick party games for adults are the short-round options already tagged Quick on the live games list — mostly no-props, mostly easy to teach.

Host on a phone, let friends join with a code, and pick the energy you actually have: a story, a vote, a would-you-rather style lock, or a timed naming burst. Alcohol is never required. A timer, when there is one, is for the game — never a reason to race a drink.

You must be of legal drinking age in your location. Passing is allowed. Pause or stop rather than recording a choice someone refused.
