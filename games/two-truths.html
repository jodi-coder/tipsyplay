🌀 Two Truths & a Twist

👥 3–10🧰 None😌 easy📱 phone
Non-alcoholic alternative (always fine): Fooled guessers give you a point.
Host Game

You must be of legal drinking age in your location.

Setup

  1. Host a room and let friends join with the code.
  2. The app gives the storyteller a topic. They write two truths and one fib about it.
  3. Keep the lines short enough to read on a phone.

Rules

  1. The storyteller marks which line is the fib. The others don't see that yet.
  2. Everyone else taps the line they think is fake.
  3. A wrong guess is one small sip, or you give the storyteller a point.
  4. One optional sip per guess. No follow-up penalties.

Variations

  • Points only: turn on non-alcoholic mode.
  • All three lines have to be about this week.
  • The storyteller gets ten seconds to sell it before the vote.
