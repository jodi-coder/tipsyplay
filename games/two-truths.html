What is Two Truths & a Twist?

Two Truths & a Twist is TipsyPlay's take on the classic icebreaker. The storyteller writes three lines — two true, one fib. Everyone else guesses which line is fake. Fooled guessers hand over a point. It is an easy party game for adults that works for 3–10 players with no props.

Every phone sees the three lines so remote joiners and couch hangouts stay in sync. Host a room, pass the code, and rotate storytellers.

Setup

  1. One person hosts a room and reads the join code out loud. Everyone joins on their own phone.
  2. The game picks a storyteller and shows a topic, such as "a job you almost took" or "a trip that went sideways".
  3. Keep it light. Pick stories you are happy for the whole table to hear.

Rules

  1. The storyteller types three short lines about the topic: two true, one invented.
  2. Everyone else reads the lines on their own phone and locks in the one they think is the fib. Passing is always fine.
  3. When every guess is in, the phones reveal the fib.
  4. Anyone who was fooled takes one small optional drink or hands the storyteller a point. Nothing stacks.
  5. The next player becomes the storyteller with a new topic.

Variations

  • Theme night: every topic is about travel, food or school.
  • Story time: after the reveal, the storyteller tells the full story behind one true line.
  • Couples round: partners write lines about each other and the table guesses.

Non-alcoholic option

Turn on points only: fooled guessers give the storyteller a point instead of drinking. Points are a full way to play, and passing is always welcome. Legal age only — play responsibly.
