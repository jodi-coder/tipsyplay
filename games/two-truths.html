What is Two Truths & a Twist?

Two Truths & a Twist is TipsyPlay's take on the classic icebreaker. The storyteller writes three lines — two true, one fib. Everyone else guesses which line is fake. Fooled guessers hand over a point. It is an easy party game for adults that works for 3–10 players with no props.

Every phone sees the three lines so remote joiners and couch hangouts stay in sync. Host a room, pass the code, and rotate storytellers.

Setup

  1. Host a room. Friends join with the code.
  2. Storyteller prepares three short lines.
  3. Agree that fibs stay playful and kind.

Rules

  1. The storyteller shares two truths and one fib.
  2. The group guesses the fib.
  3. Fooled guessers give the storyteller a point — or take one small optional sip if your table prefers that house rule.
  4. Nothing stacks. Next storyteller starts clean.

Variations

  • Theme night: only travel stories, only food, only childhood.
  • Rapid fire: thirty seconds to write the three lines.
  • Team guess: pairs submit one shared answer.

Non-alcoholic option

Fooled guessers give you a point. Soft drinks welcome. Legal age only — play safe.

Why it works as a party game for adults

Two Truths & a Twist is an icebreaker that scales from three friends to a full living room. Points replace pressure, so drinkers and non-drinkers share the same scoreboard. Pair it with Story Chain when the table wants more narrative games for adults, or jump to a card drinking game when you are ready for a deck.

Remind everyone that fibs should stay playful. If a topic feels too personal, pass and pick a lighter line. Responsible play always beats a forced reveal.

FAQ

Can I play without drinking?

Yes. Fooled guessers can give the storyteller a point. An optional sip is only if your table prefers that house rule. You may decline both a drink and any alternative, with no replacement action.

How many people can play?

3–10 players. No props required.

What if a prompt feels too personal?

Pass and pick a lighter line. Fibs should stay playful, and a forced reveal is not the point.

Do I need an app download?

No. Host a room, share the code, and play in the browser.
