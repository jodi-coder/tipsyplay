    How Vault Traitor works

    The host opens a room and friends join on their own phones. The game draws a secret four-digit code and quietly picks one Traitor (two with 6 or more players). Everyone else is a Codebreaker.

    Take turns guessing. After each guess everyone sees how many digits are exact and how many are right but misplaced. Traitors can secretly bend that feedback twice per game. You have 10 guesses.

    After each clue there's a 30-second debate. Anyone can call a secret vote. A majority catches a Traitor, but a wrong call costs two guesses and a tie costs one.

    Play responsibly

    Legal drinking age only. Every drink has a no-drink alternative, and "No drinking" turns every drink into one. Pace yourself.
