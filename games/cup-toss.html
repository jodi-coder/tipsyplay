What is Cup Toss Challenge?

Cup Toss Challenge is a skill party game for adults. You toss a ping-pong ball toward water-filled cups while TipsyPlay runs turns, challenges, and the scoreboard. Nobody drinks from the game cups — the liquid is only there so the ball lands with a splash and a clear hit.

It works for 2–8 players and is ideal when you want movement without a drinking ladder. Host on one phone, keep score in the app, and let the winner pick the next song.

Setup

  1. Fill cups with water only. Keep drinks for people separate from game cups.
  2. Host a room so turns and scores stay honest.
  3. Agree on distance and whether rebounds count.

Rules

  1. On your turn, toss for the cups shown in the challenge.
  2. Hits score points in the app. Misses do not invent extra sips.
  3. Follow any on-screen challenge the host enables.
  4. The night ends when you finish the agreed rounds or hit a score goal.

Variations

  • Team relay: alternate tosses across two teams.
  • Weak hand only for one bonus round.
  • Winner picks the playlist for the next game.

Keep spare water for refills so game cups stay water-only. Separate personal drinks from the toss triangle so nobody confuses a game cup with a beverage. That separation is the whole point of this party game for adults: skill and scoreboard, not forced sips.

Non-alcoholic option

It is already points-only. Winner picks the next song. Legal age reminder still applies if anyone chooses to drink separately — play safe.
