🥤 Cup Toss Challenge

👥 2–8🥤 Cups🎯 medium📱 phone
Non-alcoholic alternative (always fine): It's already points-only. The winner picks the next song.
Host Game

You must be of legal drinking age in your location.

Setup

  1. Set out cups of water and one ping-pong ball. The water is not a drink.
  2. Host a room so the phone can run the order.
  3. Agree on a throwing line. One toss per turn.

Rules

  1. The app shows whose turn it is and a challenge, like off-hand or one bounce.
  2. They toss once. Hit is 1 point. Miss is 0.
  3. Nobody drinks from the game cups. Points only.
  4. The winner at the end picks the next song.

Variations

  • Team cups: alternate players, one shared score.
  • After two hits, take one step back.
  • A seated round: every toss happens from a chair.
