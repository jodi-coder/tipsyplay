What is Slow Drip?

Slow Drip hides a phrase behind blanks and reveals it one letter at a time. Anyone can buzz in to solve it, but the moment you buzz, everyone else gets 3 seconds to bet on whether you will get it. It is a race and a bluff at once: buzz early and the table doubts you, wait too long and someone else takes the round.

Best with 3–10 players, each on their own phone. No props required.

Setup

  1. Host a room. Friends join with the code or the QR.
  2. Pick No-alcohol mode if the table wants dares only.
  3. Start. The host's phone runs the round; everyone plays on their own screen.

Rules

  1. Drip. The board shows the category and the phrase as blanks. Spaces and punctuation always show, and about 30% of the letters start revealed. Every 10 seconds one more letter drops.
  2. Buzz. Tap SOLVE when you think you know it. The first buzz wins and the drip clock freezes.
  3. Bet. Everyone else has 3 seconds to pick SOLVE or FAIL in secret. No pick in time counts as FAIL. You can also tap Pass: no bet, no drink either way.
  4. Hot seat. The solver has 10 seconds to type the phrase. Out of time counts as wrong.
  5. Payout. Right answer: the solver and the SOLVE bets are safe, and the FAIL bets take a drink. Wrong answer: the solver and the SOLVE bets take a drink, and the FAIL bets are safe.
  6. Penalty box. A wrong solver cannot buzz again until 2 more letters have dropped. The drip picks up where it stopped.
  7. Round end. A correct solve ends the round. If every letter drops with no solve, the phrase is shown and nobody drinks. The scoreboard counts rounds solved.

Drinking rules

  • Drinking is always optional. Every drink banner shows who drinks, why, and a no-drink alternative.
  • At most one drink per player per round. Any further penalty that round is the alternative.
  • Passing is always fine, with no penalty.

Non-alcoholic option

Turn on No-alcohol mode in the lobby and every penalty is a quick dare, such as a 5-second statue pose. Soft drinks welcome. Of legal drinking age only — house code.

FAQ

Can I play Slow Drip without drinking?

Yes. Turn on No-alcohol mode in the lobby and every penalty becomes a quick no-drink dare. Even with it off, each drink banner shows a no-drink alternative, and you may decline both. Passing is always fine.

How many people can play?

3 to 10 players, each on their own phone.

What happens if I do not bet in time?

A missed bet counts as FAIL, and your screen says so. If you would rather sit a bet out, tap Pass during the 3-second window: no drink either way.

What if two people buzz at the same moment?

The first buzz the server receives wins. The other player sees who is solving and joins the betting.

How exact does my answer need to be?

Capital letters, punctuation, accents and extra spaces are ignored, and about one typo per 8 letters is allowed. Every word of the phrase has to be there.

Can I try it on my own?

Yes. Practice with 3 bots runs on one phone with no room and no connection.
