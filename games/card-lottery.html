What is Card Lottery?

Card Lottery is a fast card drinking game for adults that needs no physical deck. The phone is the dealer. Everyone secretly locks red or black. When all guesses are in, TipsyPlay flips a color. A wrong guess is one small optional sip — or a tally mark if you prefer a points night. Passing is always fine. One sip per round, maximum. No chugging and no bonus sips that stack into later rounds.

It works for 2–8 players and is one of the easiest card drinking games on TipsyPlay. Host a room, share the code, and friends join from their own phones. You can set 5, 10, 15, or 20 rounds in the lobby and turn on non-alcoholic mode for a pure tally race.

Setup

  1. No physical deck. The phone is the dealer.
  2. Host a room. Friends join with the code.
  3. Each round is one guess. Nothing stacks into the next round.

Rules

  1. Everyone secretly picks red or black.
  2. When all guesses are in, the phone flips a color.
  3. A wrong guess is one small sip, or a tally mark. Passing is always fine.
  4. One sip per round, maximum. No chugging and no bonus sips.

Variations

  • Points night: turn on non-alcoholic mode and race the tally only.
  • Best of five, then the leader picks the next snack.
  • Say your color out loud after you lock it, not before.

Non-alcoholic option

A wrong guess earns a tally mark. Water and soft drinks are always welcome. Legal drinking age only — see the house code.
