What is Card Lottery?

Card Lottery is a fast card drinking game for adults that needs no physical deck. The phone is the dealer. Everyone secretly locks red or black, or passes the guess. When all guesses are in, TipsyPlay flips a color. Correct guesses score points. A miss does not add a point, and the app does not keep a miss tally. Any drink is one small optional drink — never a penalty saved for later. Pass skips that color guess; it is not a “no drink” button. One drink per round, maximum. No chugging and no bonus drinks that stack into later rounds.

It works for 2–8 players and is one of the easiest card drinking games on TipsyPlay. Host a room, share the code, and friends join from their own phones. You can set 5, 10, 15, or 20 rounds in the lobby.

Setup

Variations

  • Points night: keep score on correct guesses; any drink stays optional.
  • Best of five, then the leader picks the next snack.
  • Say your color out loud after you lock it, not before.

FAQ

Can I play without drinking?

Yes. Alcohol is never required. Soft drinks count. You may decline both a drink and any alternative, with no replacement action. Pause or stop instead of recording a refused action.

What does a wrong guess actually do?

The score counts a correct color. A miss does not add a point, and the app does not keep a separate miss tally. Any drink follows house policy: one small optional drink, not a penalty saved for later.

What does Pass do?

Red, Black, and Pass are for the color guess. Pass skips that guess. It is not a separate “no drink” button.

Do I need a real deck?

No. The phone is the dealer. Friends join with a code from their own phones.
