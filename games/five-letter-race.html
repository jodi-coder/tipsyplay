How a race works
  1. The host starts the race. A 3-2-1 countdown plays, then everyone sees their own empty board.
  2. Everyone is hunting the same secret word, with six guesses each. Other players see only your progress, not your letters.
  3. Solving it ranks you first, by fewest guesses and then fastest. Then come players who used all six guesses.
  4. When everyone is done, the word is revealed and every board is shown side by side.
  5. Drinking is optional. A drink card is only an offer, with a no-drink pose and Pass beside it.
Play responsibly: passing is always fine, and the default has no penalty at all.
