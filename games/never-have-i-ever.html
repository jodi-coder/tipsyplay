    How Never Have I Ever works on TipsyPlay

    Never Have I Ever is a party game for adults. One person hosts a room and shares a join link. Friends open it, type a name, and land in the same game. The host picks Mild, Classic, or Spicy, plus a game length such as 15 rounds.

    Each round puts one original line on every phone. A short countdown is only for answering. Tap I HAVE or NEVER once. No second confirm. If you said I have, take one small sip or hold a 3-second pose. Water counts. Nothing stacks, and there is no timer on the sip.

    When the rounds are done, the table gets playful awards such as The Saint and Most Chaotic. Those are about who said yes, not about who drank. You can also type your own line for the next round. The packs are there if you don't.

    Non-alcoholic option

    The pose is on screen whenever a yes lands. Skip the sip and hold it for three seconds. Passing is always fine.

    Play responsibly

    Legal drinking age only. Sips are optional and small. No chugging and no pressure. See the house code. Looking for another game? Open Who's Most Likely or the full games list.
