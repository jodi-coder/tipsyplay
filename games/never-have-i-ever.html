    How Never Have I Ever works on TipsyPlay

    Never Have I Ever is a party game for adults. One person hosts a room and shares a code. Friends open it, type a name, and land in the same game. The host picks Mild, Classic, Spicy, or Most likely, plus a game length such as 15 rounds.

    Each round puts one original line on every phone. A short countdown is only for answering. Tap I HAVE or NEVER once. No second confirm. If you said I have, take one small drink or hold a 3-second pose. Nothing stacks, and there is no timer on the drink.

    When the rounds are done, the table gets playful awards such as The Saint and Most Chaotic. Those are about who said yes, not about who drank. You can also type your own line for the next round. The packs are there if you don't.

    Most likely mode

    Pick Most likely and each round asks "Who's most likely to…". Everyone taps a friend on their own phone instead of I HAVE or NEVER, and you can't vote for yourself. The person with the most votes (everyone tied) gets the optional drink or pose, and may explain themselves. Classic, Spicy and Couples question packs are included. Awards in this mode are about who got mentioned, not who drank.

    Non-alcoholic option

    The pose is on screen whenever a yes lands. Skip the drink and hold it for three seconds. Passing is always fine.

    Play responsibly

    Legal drinking age only. Drinking is optional and small. No chugging and no pressure. See the house code. Looking for another game? Open Two Truths & a Twist or the full games list.

    FAQ

    Can I play without drinking?

    Yes. If you tap I HAVE, the screen offers one small optional drink or a 3-second pose. Skip the drink. You may also decline both a drink and the pose — you do not owe a replacement action. If a screen only offers an action you have declined, pause or stop instead of recording a choice you did not make.

    How many people can play?

    2–12 players. One person hosts; everyone else joins from their own phone.

    Is there a Pass button?

    Pass sits with I HAVE and NEVER. A pass is not a yes, adds no drink, and does not stack. You may still decline a drink and any alternative.

    What is Most likely mode?

    A mode the host picks at setup. Each round asks who in the room is most likely to do something. Everyone votes for a friend on their own phone, and the person with the most votes gets the optional drink or pose. You can't vote for yourself, and Pass is always fine.

    Does anything stack?

    No drink penalties stack. Yeses are stories, not a drink score, and there is no timer on the drink.

    Do I need to download an app?

    No. Host a room on TipsyPlay and share the code. Play in the browser on the phones you already have.
