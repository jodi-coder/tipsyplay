🙈 Never Have I Ever

👥 3–12🧰 None😌 chill📱 phone
Non-alcoholic alternative (always fine): fold a finger or score a point instead of sipping. Toggle “Non-alcoholic mode” in the game to change all instructions.
Host Game

You must be of legal drinking age in your location.

Setup

  1. One person taps Host Game. Everyone else joins with the code on their own phone.
  2. Everyone grabs a drink of their choice — water and mocktails count.
  3. Pick a spice level everyone is comfy with. When in doubt, go milder.

Rules

  1. The prompt shows on every phone.
  2. If you have done it, you may take one small sip — or do the alt (fold a finger / score a point).
  3. Totally opt-in: you can pass on any card, no explanation needed.
  4. Tap I have or Never on your phone. One sip per prompt, max — no extra penalties.

Variations

  • Five Fingers: start with 5 fingers up; last person with fingers up wins. No drinks needed.
  • Story Time: anyone who “has” can share the story (optional) for a bonus point.
  • Team Mode: split into two teams and tally points.
