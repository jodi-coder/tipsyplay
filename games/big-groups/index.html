Party games for big groups

A bigger table needs games that let everyone act on their own phone — not a single device passed around the couch. These party games for adults are the ones for 10 or more players.

Pick a familiar never-have-I-ever, a most-likely-to vote, a drawing round, or a card drinking game with no stacked drink penalties. Alcohol-free play belongs at the same table. If a screen only offers an action someone has declined, pause or stop instead of recording it.

You must be of legal drinking age in your location. Know your limits, plan a ride, and read the house code.
