Party games for big groups

A bigger table needs games that let everyone act on their own phone — not a single device passed around the couch. These party games for adults are built for bigger tables: most take up to 10 or 12 players, and Vault Traitor takes up to 8.

Pick a familiar never-have-I-ever, a most-likely-to vote, a drawing round, or a card drinking game with no stacked drink penalties. Alcohol-free play belongs at the same table. If a screen only offers an action someone has declined, pause or stop instead of recording it.

Know your limits, plan a ride, and read the house code.

You must be of legal drinking age in your location.

A hidden phrase appears one letter at a time. Buzz in to solve it while everyone else bets on SOLVE or FAIL.

Vault Traitor

Crack a four-digit code together while one or two hidden Traitors bend the clues.

3–8 players

Never Have I Ever

Each phone joins with a code. One small drink or a 3-second pose. Nothing stacks. Includes a Most likely mode where everyone votes for a friend.

2–12 players
Host

Story Chain

Build a ridiculous story one sentence at a time.

3–10 players
Host

Category Countdown

Race the ring timer in Spotlight, or survive Hot Potato around the table. Optional TV board.

3–10 players
Host

Two Truths & a Twist

Share two truths and one fib. The group guesses the fib.

3–10 players
Host

Sketchy Guess

Draw a secret word on your phone. The sketch shows up for everyone else. Points only.

4–12 players
Host

Rule Maker

Cards create silly table rules. One optional drink per slip — no escalation, no king's cup.

4–10 players
Host

Split Decision

Lock A or B, then predict how many friends picked A. Keeps score in points.

2–10 players
Host

King's Cup

Each phone joins with a code. Every card is one small drink or a non-alcoholic alternative. Nothing stacks.

2–10 players
Host

Five Letter Frenzy

One secret word on one shared grid. Six guesses for the table. A miss is one small drink, a 5-second pose, or Pass.

3–8 players
Host
