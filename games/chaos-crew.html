How Chaos Crew works

Chaos Crew is a cooperative phone party game for adults. One person hosts. Friends scan a QR code or type the four-letter room code. A fictional mission shows two to four roles with visible quotas. Everyone secretly volunteers for one role. The reveal shows how the crew landed. Perfect coverage earns one shared star.

Passing is always fine. A pass during a pick removes that person and restarts the attempt so nobody is stuck with a stale choice. Nothing stacks, and nobody is the loser. Points, which here are team stars, are the default. A small sip, a wave, or nothing are all fine after the reveal, and both the drink and the wave can be skipped.

Legal drinking age only. See the house code, or try Split Decision and Who's Most Likely.

FAQ

Can I play without drinking?

Yes. The score is team stars out of 8. After a reveal, a small sip, a wave, or nothing are all fine, and both the drink and the wave can be skipped. You may decline both a drink and any alternative, with no replacement.

Is somebody the loser?

No. Nobody is the loser. Points here are team stars.

What does Pass do?

Pass is for this game’s pick, not a drink button. A pass during a pick removes that person and restarts the attempt once. Declining a drink still follows the house code.

How do we join?

One person hosts. Friends scan a QR or type the four-letter room code.
