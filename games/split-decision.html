How Split Decision works

Split Decision is a phone party game for adults. One person hosts. Friends scan a QR code or type the four-letter room code. Everyone secretly picks A or B, then predicts how many of the others chose A. You never score for matching the majority. You score for reading the room.

Passing is always fine. The first pass sits you out of that round. A pass on the forecast keeps your pick in the count and scores zero for the guess. Nothing stacks. Points are the default. A small drink or nothing is fine after the reveal. Instead of a drink, whoever picked the minority side takes a bow, and the bow can be skipped too.

Legal drinking age only. See the house code, or try Never Have I Ever and Two Truths & a Twist.

FAQ

Can I play without drinking?

Yes. The game keeps score in points. After a reveal, a line may offer an optional small drink, or you can skip it and let whoever picked the minority side take a bow — and both the drink and the bow can be skipped. You may decline both a drink and any alternative, with no replacement action.

Is this a points-only game?

It keeps score in points. After a reveal, you may take a small optional drink, take a bow if you picked the minority side, or do nothing — and you can skip both the drink and the bow. Alcohol is never required.

What does Pass do?

There is a Pass control for this game’s pick, and the effect changes by step — it is not a drink button. A pass on the first pick sits you out; a pass on the forecast keeps your pick and scores 0 for the guess. Declining a drink still follows the house code.

How do we join?

One person hosts. Friends scan a QR code or type the four-letter room code.
