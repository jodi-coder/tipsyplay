Rules

Lock A or B. Then guess how many of the other eligible players picked A. Exact is 2 points, one away is 1. With only two eligible players, exact is 1 and a miss is 0. Pass on the first pick sits you out. Pass on the forecast keeps your pick and scores 0 for the guess. Eight rounds. A tie shares the win.

How Split Decision works

Split Decision is a phone party game for adults. One person hosts. Friends scan a QR code or type the four-letter room code. Everyone secretly picks A or B, then predicts how many of the others chose A. You never score for matching the majority. You score for reading the room.

Passing is always fine. The first pass sits you out of that round. A pass on the forecast keeps your pick in the count and scores zero for the guess. Nothing stacks. Points are the default. A small sip, a wave, or nothing are all fine after the reveal, and both the drink and the wave can be skipped.

Legal drinking age only. See the house code, or try Never Have I Ever and Vibe Checkpoint.
