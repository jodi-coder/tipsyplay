    Rules

    Pick A or B before the 20-second clock runs out. Then guess how many of the other players picked A. Your own pick never counts in that number. Exact is 2 points, one away is 1, anything else is 0. Pass, or no answer in time, scores 0 for that question. Eight rounds. A tie shares the win.

    How Split Decision works

    Split Decision is a phone party game for adults. One person hosts. Friends scan a QR code or type the four-letter room code. Everyone secretly picks A or B, then predicts how many of the others chose A. You never score for matching the majority. You score for reading the room.

    Passing is always fine. Pass, or let the clock run, and you score zero for that question. Nothing stacks. Points are the whole game. A small drink or nothing is fine after the reveal. Instead of a drink, whoever picked the minority side takes a bow, and the bow can be skipped too.

    Legal drinking age only. See the house code, or try Never Have I Ever and Two Truths & a Twist.
