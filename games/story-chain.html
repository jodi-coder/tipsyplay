What is Story Chain?

Story Chain is a no-props party game for adults. In each round, every phone can type one short sentence or pass, and the submitted lines show for the room. TipsyPlay puts the prompt on every phone so nobody loses the thread. Themes and a shared story make it funny fast — without needing cards or cups.

It shines with 3–10 people at a house hang, pre-game, or campus night in. Host starts a room, friends join with a code, and the chain grows until the table decides the tale is done.

Setup

  1. Host a room and share the join code.
  2. Agree on a light theme if you want one (vacation gone wrong, cooking disaster, etc.).
  3. Phones stay unlocked so everyone can type a short sentence or pass during the same round.

Rules

  1. Each phone may type exactly one short sentence, or pass, in the same round.
  2. Keep it party-friendly and keep the story moving.
  3. If someone freezes or breaks the vibe rule your table set, they can take one small optional sip — or the alternative below.
  4. Nothing stacks. The next round starts clean.

Variations

  • Genre night: only sci-fi, only sports, or only cooking shows.
  • Emoji-only endings for the last word of each sentence.
  • Reader's chair: one person narrates the full story at the end.

Non-alcoholic option

Do a 5-second dramatic pose instead of sipping. Points and poses are a full way to play. Legal age only — play safe.

FAQ

Can I play without drinking?

Yes. Alcohol is never required. The live page offers a 5-second dramatic pose instead of a sip. You may decline both a drink and any alternative — no replacement action is owed. Pause or stop rather than recording a choice someone refused.

Do we take turns around the table?

Not as an enforced spoken circle. Each phone can type one short sentence or pass during the same round, and submitted lines show for the room.

How many people can play?

Story Chain is for 3–10 players. Host a room and share the code; everyone plays on their own phone.

Do I need cards or paper?

No. It is a no-props phone game. Host with a code; every phone sees the shared prompt.
