Syracuse University

Why it ranks: Marshall Street and a basketball-campus reputation in a snowy city put Syracuse on community lists. This note is unofficial.

Syracuse University sits on a hill above the city, and Marshall Street is the short commercial block at the edge of campus where students actually bump into each other. It is restaurants and rooms more than a district. Basketball at the Dome is the winter tradition that fills the calendar the way football does elsewhere. Snow is not a rumour. It changes every plan that looked easy at noon.

The city of Syracuse has its own downtown, an Armory Square night, and more food than the campus block. Students who only ever see Marshall Street are using one room of the house. A good night might be the game and a ride downtown, or it might be Marshall Street pizza and residence. Both are enough. Orange weekends do not come with a minimum.

Centro buses connect campus and downtown until they do not. Late, or in a storm, rideshare is the way down the hill and back up it. Walking down is a different sport from walking up. Stay with friends, keep a drink in sight, and call the car before the steps feel like a bad idea. A small sip is optional. Passing is always fine.
