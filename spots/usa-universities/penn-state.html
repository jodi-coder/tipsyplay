Penn State

Why it ranks: A self-contained college town and huge football weekends put Penn State on community lists. TipsyPlay does not speak for the university.

Penn State in State College is the rare campus that is the town. Downtown sits against the university, so dinner, a room, and residence can be one walk in September and a long cold walk in January. Football weekends at Beaver Stadium pull alumni, traffic, and a temporary city of tents. That weekend is famous. It is also optional, and the town is still there on the Sundays after.

Student traditions here include the game, THON and other big service events, and a downtown that students treat as a living room. You can participate in the living-room version for an hour. Late kitchens and pizza count as culture. Nobody is issuing grades for how many doors you opened, and a designated driver is a more useful tradition than any of them.

CATA buses loop campus and downtown and get overwhelmed when the stadium lets out. Rideshare and a pre-set meeting point beat improvising on a game night. Stay with your group, keep a drink in sight, and start home when the plan is complete rather than when the street is. A small sip is optional. Passing is always fine.
