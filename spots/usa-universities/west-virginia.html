West Virginia University

Why it ranks: High Street in a mountain college town puts West Virginia on community lists. TipsyPlay is not affiliated with the university.

West Virginia University in Morgantown is a hillside campus above a small downtown. High Street is the name students use for the rooms and restaurants at the bottom of that hill. Football weekends change the scale of the town. The rest of the year it is a compact college main street, which is either cozy or repetitive depending on your week. The mountains are the scenery. They are not a midnight plan.

Campus traditions lean on the game, student events, and the fact that most of the social map fits in one walk when the weather agrees. PRT trains on campus are a daytime quirk more than a nightlife system. A solid night is High Street dinner and a single room, then the ride up. You do not need to close the street to have been out.

Mountain Line buses help before the evening gets thin. Late, rideshare or a designated driver is how you handle the hill, especially in winter when the walk looks shorter than it feels. Stay with your group, watch your drink, and start up while you are still glad you came out. A small sip is optional. Passing is always fine.
