San Diego State

Why it ranks: A college area inside a beach city puts San Diego State on community lists. The beaches are a ride away, and this page is unofficial.

San Diego State sits in the College Area, a neighbourhood of student apartments, fast meals, and rooms that exist because the campus is there. It is not on the beach. Pacific Beach, Ocean Beach, and downtown are rides, which surprises visitors who pictured a campus on the sand. The local night is often closer and simpler: food on the avenue and people you already know from class.

Football and basketball give the school its louder weekends. The rest of the term is a college neighbourhood inside a city that has better tacos than most campuses deserve. Use that. A beach afternoon can end before it becomes a long night, and a College Area dinner can be the whole plan. School spirit does not require a coastal bar crawl.

The trolley and buses reach parts of San Diego and still leave you planning the last leg. Rideshare is the ordinary way between campus, a beach neighbourhood, and downtown. Stay together, watch your drink, and do not stitch three beach towns together after dark. A small sip is optional. Passing is always fine.
