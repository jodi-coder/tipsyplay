Indiana University

Why it ranks: Kirkwood Avenue in a classic college town puts Indiana on community lists. Basketball is the famous tradition. This page is unofficial.

Indiana University in Bloomington is a walkable college town with Kirkwood Avenue as the student commercial street. Campus, downtown restaurants, and a square of rooms sit close together, which is the luxury of the place. You can have dinner and be back at a residence without a freeway. Basketball at Assembly Hall is the tradition people travel for. Football has its weekends too. Neither one is a homework assignment.

The town also has music, a real restaurant habit, and the limestone-campus look that makes an ordinary evening feel like you went somewhere. Little 500 weekend and other campus spectacles swell the sidewalks. On a normal night, Kirkwood is one meal and maybe one door. You will not unlock a secret Bloomington by staying later than your friends want to.

Campus and city buses help until the evening quiets down. After that, rideshare or a short walk with your group is the whole transit puzzle, and the walk should be one you would still take if you were tired. Stay together, keep a drink in sight, and head back when the game or the dinner is over. A small sip is optional. Passing is always fine.
