Arizona State

Why it ranks: Mill Avenue and a huge Tempe campus put Arizona State on community lists. Heat is the real scheduler. This page is unofficial.

Arizona State in Tempe is a huge campus with Mill Avenue as the old main street beside it. Restaurants, patios, and rooms line a walk that feels like the edge of campus rather than a separate city. Phoenix is right there and still a ride away. The thing that schedules the night is the heat. In summer, patios make more sense after dark, and water is a more useful order than people admit.

Football and a very large student body are why the school appears on lists. Tempe also has shows, a lake, and plenty of nights that are just dinner. You can live next to Mill Avenue and treat it as a place to eat. Game days add crowds. They do not add a requirement. A light-rail ride to a different neighbourhood is sometimes the better use of the evening.

Valley Metro rail stops near campus and is one of the few student nights in this list with a train that might actually match the plan. It still does not run forever. Rideshare covers the rest, including the trip toward Phoenix or Scottsdale if that was the idea. Stay with your group, watch your drink, and respect the heat. A small sip is optional. Passing is always fine.
