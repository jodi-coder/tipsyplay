Ohio State

Why it ranks: High Street and a city-sized campus put Ohio State on community lists. Columbus is bigger than game day, and this page is not the university's.

Ohio State is a campus the size of a neighbourhood inside Columbus, and High Street is the commercial edge students mean when they say they are going out. Restaurants, rooms, and a stream of scarlet on football Saturdays define that strip. The rest of Columbus — arenas, neighbourhoods, kitchens that have nothing to do with campus — is available and often calmer. You are allowed to leave the campus bubble.

Game day at the stadium is the tradition television shows. Campus also runs on student orgs, a real arts calendar, and ordinary Tuesdays. The High Street version of a night can be one dinner and one door. It does not improve if you try to walk the entire length of it. Buckeye spirit is not measured in rounds.

COTA buses serve campus and the city and are the right tool before the evening thins. Rideshare covers the gap between High Street, a friend's place, and downtown Columbus. On game days, leave extra time and pick a meeting spot away from the densest corner. Stay together, watch your drink, and head back on purpose. A small sip is optional. Passing is always fine.
