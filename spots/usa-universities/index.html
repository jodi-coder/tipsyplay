Best party spots: USA universities

TipsyPlay community picks — based on nightlife reputation. Disagree? Debate below. These pages are unofficial and not affiliated with the schools. This order is not a Niche ranking.

These notes are unofficial. TipsyPlay is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or speaking for the school.

  1. 1

    Florida State University

    Tallahassee on a football weekend, with College Town nearby and a ride back to campus.

  2. 2

    Tulane University

    Uptown New Orleans campus life, with a streetcar toward the music and a ride when it stops.

  3. 3

    UW–Madison

    State Street between the capitol and the lake, with football Saturdays that change the whole isthmus.

  4. 4

    UC Santa Barbara

    Isla Vista bikes, a beach campus, and a night that should stay smaller than its legends.

  5. 5

    University of Alabama

    Tuscaloosa on a Saturday, with the Strip near campus and a ride when the stadium lets out.

  6. 6

    University of Georgia

    Athens downtown and a campus that shares the sidewalk with a music town.

  7. 7

    Penn State

    State College on a football weekend, with downtown a walk from campus and a ride if the walk wins.

  8. 8

    Ohio State

    High Street in Columbus, a huge campus, and a night that can just be the city.

  9. 9

    Syracuse University

    Marshall Street, a hilltop campus, and basketball nights that the weather does not cancel.

  10. 10

    USC

    A campus next to downtown Los Angeles, where the night usually starts with a ride.

  11. 11

    West Virginia University

    Morgantown's High Street, a hillside campus, and football weekends in a small city.

  12. 12

    University of Miami

    Coral Gables campus life, with the beach as a ride rather than a backyard.

  13. 13

    San Diego State

    The College Area in San Diego, with tacos nearby and the beach as a separate ride.

  14. 14

    Arizona State

    Tempe's Mill Avenue, a huge campus, and nights that start when the sidewalks cool down.

  15. 15

    Indiana University

    Bloomington's Kirkwood Avenue, a walkable campus, and basketball as the loud tradition.

You must be of legal drinking age in your location.
