USC

Why it ranks: A campus beside downtown Los Angeles puts USC on community lists for football and a city night. The university is not involved in this page.

The University of Southern California sits just south of downtown Los Angeles, which sounds convenient until you try to do the city without a car. Campus has its own football Saturdays at the Coliseum, student events, and a village of restaurants at the gates. The rest of LA — a show, a neighbourhood dinner, a friend's place — is a ride away, and that ride is the plan rather than a detail.

People lump USC in with a generic Los Angeles night. The more accurate version is one neighbourhood you chose on purpose. Downtown is the closest. Santa Monica, Koreatown, or a concert across town are separate trips. Game day adds traffic that makes 'we'll just go' a poor strategy. Dinner near campus and an early night is a respectable use of Los Angeles.

Metro rail has expanded the map and still will not be your door-to-door answer after midnight. Rideshare is the ordinary link, and it is worth ordering before a stadium crowd does. Stay with your group, watch your drink, and do not improvise a long walk off campus late. A small sip is optional. Passing is always fine.
