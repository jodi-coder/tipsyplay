Florida State University

Why it ranks: Football weekends and a college-town strip put Florida State on community lists. TipsyPlay is not affiliated with the school.

Florida State University turns Tallahassee into a college town with a capital attached. Campus traditions are loudest on football Saturdays, when garnet and gold take over the sidewalks around Doak Campbell Stadium. The rest of the calendar is classes, student events, and nights that do not need a stadium to justify themselves. You can love game day and still spend it at the game rather than at a pre-game that has no clock.

College Town and the blocks students actually use are a short trip from campus, with restaurants and rooms aimed at the term-time crowd. Visitors often expect one endless strip. The more pleasant version is dinner, the event you came for, and a limit. The heat decides the patio question for half the year. A non-alcoholic drink in a loud room is a normal order, not a statement.

StarMetro buses help by day. On football nights and late weekends, rideshare is the realistic way between campus, College Town, and a place to stay. Book it before the stadium empties if you can. Stay with your group, watch your drink, and leave when the plan you made is finished. A small sip is optional. Passing is always fine.
