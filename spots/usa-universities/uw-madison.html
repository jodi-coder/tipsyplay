UW–Madison

Why it ranks: State Street and a football-and-lakes reputation put UW–Madison on community lists. Big unsanctioned street gatherings have drawn police attention. This page is unofficial.

The University of Wisconsin–Madison sits on an isthmus between two lakes, and State Street is the walk everyone names, running from campus toward the capitol. Restaurants, student bars, and shops share those blocks with people who are not in school. Football Saturdays at Camp Randall redraw the town. They are a tradition you can attend as a game, not as an all-day obligation.

Spring has sometimes brought large unsanctioned gatherings that police and the university have warned against. Treat any 'the whole street is invited' plan as a reason to choose something else. The better Madison night is a terrace because the lakes are right there, a band, or a dinner reservation. Winter is long. A lot of the real social life is indoors and ends when you want it to.

Madison Metro covers the isthmus and gets less frequent late. Rideshare is the backup between State Street, a house, and residences when the wind is up. Stay with your group, watch your drink, and do not follow a crowd whose only plan is the crowd. A small sip is optional. Passing is always fine.
