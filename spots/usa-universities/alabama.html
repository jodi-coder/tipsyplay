University of Alabama

Why it ranks: Football Saturdays and the Strip near campus put Alabama on community lists. TipsyPlay is not part of the university, and gameday is optional.

The University of Alabama in Tuscaloosa is a football town on Saturdays and a campus town the rest of the week. The Strip, the commercial stretch near campus, is where students point visitors for restaurants and rooms. Game day at Bryant-Denny Stadium is the tradition the outside world knows. It involves traffic, tents, and a city that planned around the kickoff. You can go to the game and still decline the twelve-hour version.

On ordinary nights the Strip is dinner and a handful of doors, not a destination that requires stamina. Campus events, Greek-life calendars for those who opted in, and a lot of people who are simply studying are all happening at once. Pick the dinner. The tent circuit is not homework, and it is not mandatory school spirit.

Tuscaloosa transit helps in the daytime. When the stadium empties, rideshare is the way back to campus housing or a hotel, and ordering it early is the whole trick. Stay with your group, keep a drink in sight, and do not walk a busy game-day road as a shortcut. A small sip is optional. Passing is always fine.
