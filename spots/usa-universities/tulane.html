Tulane University

Why it ranks: An Uptown campus inside a music city puts Tulane on community lists. The school did not write this page, and Mardi Gras is not a requirement.

Tulane University sits Uptown in New Orleans, which means the campus social life and the city's social life share a map and not a schedule. Students talk about Audubon Park, campus events, and a streetcar line that points downtown toward the music. Frenchmen Street and the Quarter are a trip, not a backyard. The smart nights pick one of those and leave the other for another day.

Mardi Gras, Jazz Fest, and ordinary Thursdays are different difficulties. Festival weeks need a meeting point, a budget, and an agreement about when the group is done. A regular week can be Uptown dinner and a single room. New Orleans will offer you a go-cup and a longer walk. You can hear brass and eat well without treating the parish like a dare.

Streetcars are part of the city's charm and a poor thing to bet on after midnight. Rideshare is the reliable link between Uptown residences and a downtown show. Stay with friends, keep a drink covered and in sight, and skip the shortcut you do not know. A small sip is optional. Passing is always fine.
