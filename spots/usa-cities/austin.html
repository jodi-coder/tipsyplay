Austin

Why it ranks: Live-music reputation and Rainey Street patios keep Austin on community lists. The order here is a TipsyPlay pick, not a civic award.

Austin still introduces itself as a live-music town, and on a good night that is accurate. Sixth Street is the dense, younger strip. Rainey Street is the patio-and-house version nearby. East Austin and South Congress stretch the map toward restaurants, food trucks, and smaller stages. Festival weeks, especially SXSW, rewrite the city: wristbands, lines, and prices that do not match a normal Thursday.

The pleasant plan is one show you actually wanted to see, tacos or a food-truck stop, and a limit on how many doors you will try. Outdoor patios carry the long warm season. Brunch the next day is a local sport that does not require you to have overdone the night before. You can hear a band and eat well and still be the person who ordered a soda.

CapMetro buses and a rail line help in the daytime and early evening. After shows, rideshare is the link between downtown, Rainey, and a place to stay in another neighbourhood. Scooters look convenient and are a bad idea after drinks. Stay together, watch your glass, and set the pickup spot before you go inside. A small sip is optional. Passing is always fine.
