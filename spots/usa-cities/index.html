Best party spots: USA cities

TipsyPlay community picks — based on nightlife reputation. Disagree? Debate below. This order is not an Uber Nightlife Index and it is not a Niche ranking.

  1. 1

    Las Vegas

    Shows, late food, and a strip where walking back is rarely the best idea.

  2. 2

    New Orleans

    Brass bands, late kitchens, and a streetcar that is not a full plan after midnight.

  3. 3

    Miami

    Ocean Drive, Wynwood, and a night that works better with a ride than a long walk.

  4. 4

    Nashville

    Honky-tonks, hot chicken, and a strip where the band starts in the afternoon.

  5. 5

    Austin

    Sixth Street, Rainey patios, and a music town that also runs on tacos.

  6. 6

    New York City

    A different night in the East Village, Brooklyn, or wherever the reservation is.

  7. 7

    Chicago

    Wicker Park, Logan Square, and a blues or house room if that is the night you wanted.

  8. 8

    Atlanta

    Edgewood, Buckhead, and a city where the ride is part of the night.

  9. 9

    Scottsdale / Phoenix

    Old Town Scottsdale patios, downtown Phoenix rooms, and a desert night that cools off late.

  10. 10

    San Diego

    Gaslamp rooms, beach neighbourhoods, and tacos as a legitimate itinerary.

You must be of legal drinking age in your location.
