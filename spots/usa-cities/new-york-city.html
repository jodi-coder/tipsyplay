New York City

Why it ranks: It is too big for one rank, and people still name it for neighbourhoods that each run their own night. A community pick, not a single score.

New York does not have a party district so much as a dozen of them that ignore each other. The East Village and the Lower East Side are the classic late walk. Bushwick and Williamsburg pull the Brooklyn nights. Harlem, Washington Heights, Queens neighbourhoods, and a Midtown show each imply a different train and a different dinner. Visitors who try to sample three boroughs in one evening mostly sample the subway.

The food can outrank the bar. A late table, a slice, or a basement show is a full plan, and the city's best nights are often the ones with a start time. Comedy, jazz, dance music, and arena concerts all count. You will be offered a faster, louder version of whatever you are already doing. You can decline it and still have been in New York.

The subway is the city's real nightlife transit until it shifts to night patterns and longer waits. Check the last convenient train the way you would check a reservation. Rideshare fills gaps and gets expensive when it rains or when a big show ends. Stay with your group, watch your drink, and keep the phone charged. A small drink is optional. Passing is always fine.
