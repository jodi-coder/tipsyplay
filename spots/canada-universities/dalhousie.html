Dalhousie University

Why it ranks: A south-end Halifax campus within reach of Argyle Street puts Dalhousie on community lists. TipsyPlay is not speaking for the university.

Dalhousie University is in Halifax's south end, close enough to downtown that the city's night is part of the student map and far enough that you still make a choice to go. Campus events, a medical-and-law-and-undergrad mix, and streets of student houses give weeknights their own noise. Argyle Street and the waterfront are the downtown version: live rooms, patios, and kitchens that are not the dining hall.

The east-coast music boast belongs to the city, and students borrow it. You can hear a band, eat fish and chips, and be home without a second district. Orientation and sports weekends are busier. They are still optional. A south-end dinner and an early bus is a perfectly loyal way to spend a Dalhousie night.

Halifax Transit runs between the south end and the core until the evening thins out. After that, rideshare or a taxi is the kinder way up and down the hills, especially in bad weather. Stay with your group, keep a drink in sight, and leave when the set is over. A small sip is optional. Passing is always fine.
