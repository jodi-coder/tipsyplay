Queen's University

Why it ranks: Kingston's student neighbourhood and a loud homecoming reputation put Queen's on community lists. TipsyPlay is not ranking it for the school.

Queen's University is in Kingston, a limestone downtown with a student neighbourhood everyone still gives directions to. The University District, a tight grid near campus, is where a lot of house gatherings and student kitchens sit. Downtown Princess Street is the other walk, with restaurants and rooms that are not only for students. The city is small enough that one neighbourhood is the whole plan.

Homecoming is the weekend the town prepares for, with extra policing and a crowd that includes alumni. It is famous and it is optional. During the rest of the year the traditions people actually describe are football at Richardson Stadium, engineering frosh events, and a campus that likes its rituals more than it likes a single street. A dinner downtown and an early night is a normal Queen's evening.

Kingston Transit covers the core, and many students walk. Walking is less charming in February or when the group is tired, so rideshare is the backup between the district, downtown, and residences. Stay together, watch your drink, and skip any gathering that has no easy way to leave. A small sip is optional. Passing is always fine.
