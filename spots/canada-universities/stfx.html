St. Francis Xavier (StFX)

Why it ranks: A small Nova Scotia town and a close-knit campus put StFX on community lists. The X-Ring is the famous tradition, and it is not a drinking contest.

St. Francis Xavier University, which everyone shortens to StFX, is in Antigonish, Nova Scotia. The town is small, so campus, Main Street restaurants, and a handful of rooms are the whole map. That closeness is the charm and the limit. There is not a second district to escape to, which means a night out is either relaxed or it feels like the entire school is in the same three places.

The tradition alumni actually fly home for is the X-Ring, a December ceremony that marks the final undergraduate year. It is a campus ritual with family energy, not a bar crawl, even if dinners surround it. The rest of the year is athletics, house gatherings because the town is small, and Main Street when you want a meal that is not the dining hall. You can love the school and still keep the night short.

Transit in a town this size will not be your 1 a.m. strategy. Walk with friends on lit streets, and use rideshare or a designated driver if the group is heading to residences on the edge of campus. Stay together, watch your drink, and treat a packed house with no exit as a reason to choose dinner instead. A small sip is optional. Passing is always fine.
