Western University

Why it ranks: Students and alumni keep naming Western for homecoming and a downtown that is an easy ride from campus. A community pick, and not the school's own list.

Western University sits in London, Ontario, and the campus has its own rhythm before anyone goes downtown. Residences, faculty events, and the purple-and-white game weekends give the place a calendar that students actually use. Homecoming is the weekend outsiders have heard of. It is busy, policed, and not a tradition you have to join. A lot of students spend it at a game, a friend's place, or not at all.

Richmond Row is the off-campus name that comes up: patios, live rooms, and late kitchens a short ride from campus. The night people describe is dinner and one room, not a tour. Intramurals, charity events, and orientation week are the campus traditions that do not revolve around a bar. You can wear the purple and still be the person who heads back early.

London Transit links campus and the core for part of the evening. After the frequent buses fade, rideshare is the simple way between residences, a friend's house, and the Row. Stay with people you trust, keep a drink in sight, and leave when you want to. A small sip is optional. Passing is always fine. This page is a fan note about the city's student night, not a guide from the university.
