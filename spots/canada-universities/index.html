Best party spots: Canada universities

TipsyPlay community picks — based on nightlife reputation. Disagree? Debate below. These pages are unofficial and not affiliated with the schools. This order is not a Niche ranking.

These notes are unofficial. TipsyPlay is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or speaking for the school.

  1. 1

    Western University

    Purple weekends in London, with Richmond Row a ride from campus and homecoming that the city feels.

  2. 2

    Queen's University

    Kingston's University District, a compact downtown, and a homecoming weekend that is optional.

  3. 3

    Wilfrid Laurier University

    Uptown Waterloo patios and a campus in the middle of a walkable downtown.

  4. 4

    St. Francis Xavier (StFX)

    Antigonish is a small town, so the campus and Main Street share one evening.

  5. 5

    Bishop's University

    A small campus in Lennoxville, with Sherbrooke nearby and Quebec's later hours in the background.

  6. 6

    Acadia University

    Wolfville is walkable, with valley farms, a main street, and a campus you can cross in minutes.

  7. 7

    McGill University

    A downtown Montreal campus, with the Plateau nearby and a metro that does not run all night.

  8. 8

    University of British Columbia

    A campus on the edge of Vancouver, with downtown a bus or a ride away and the ocean in between.

  9. 9

    Dalhousie University

    South-end Halifax campus life, with Argyle and the waterfront a bus or a ride away.

  10. 10

    University of New Brunswick

    A campus on the hill in Fredericton, with downtown a walk or a ride below.

You must be of legal drinking age in your location.
