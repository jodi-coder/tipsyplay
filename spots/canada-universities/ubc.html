Rank 8 of 10

University of British Columbia

Why it ranks: A Point Grey campus with a real trip into Vancouver's night puts UBC on community lists. The school is not involved in this page.

The University of British Columbia sits on Point Grey, far enough from downtown Vancouver that the campus is its own town. Residences, the student neighbourhood, and events on the point are a full evening if you want them to be. Storm the Wall and the other campus spectacles are sports and school spirit, not a bar tradition. A lot of students never leave the point on a given night, and that is a reasonable choice.

When people do go toward the city, Granville, Main Street, and a dinner reservation are the usual reasons. Beaches near campus are a daytime plan that should stay a daytime plan. The night people describe is one neighbourhood in town, not a sweep of the North Shore. Food in Vancouver can be the entire outing, with a single room afterward or none at all.

Buses connect the point to the city and they take time. The last convenient bus is a deadline worth knowing. Rideshare back up the hill is the fallback and it is not instant on a Saturday. Stay with friends, watch your drink downtown, and do not start a walk home along the beaches in the dark. A small sip is optional. Passing is always fine.
