Rank 7 of 10

St. John's, Newfoundland

Why it ranks: St. John's was seventh on Uber's 2026 Nightlife Index. George Street's reputation is real; the rank itself is about late-night rides.

St. John's has the rare nightlife geography of a single short street doing a lot of the work. George Street is dense with rooms, cover bands, and visitors who came for exactly that concentration. Around it, Duckworth and Water Street hold restaurants, quieter bars, and the colourful downtown that photographs well before anyone goes inside. The hills are steep. What looks like a short walk at 7 can feel longer at midnight.

Music is the point people actually talk about. Pub sessions, visiting acts, and the kitchen-party reputation of Newfoundland show up in rooms that would rather you listened than rushed. Food is hearty, late plates are normal, and a festival weekend can fill every doorway. You can hear a set and eat well without treating the night like a challenge.

Metrobus serves the city, but late service is limited, and downtown hills are a bad place to guess the route. Rideshare or a taxi back up to a hotel or a neighbourhood is the plan to make first. Wind and fog are not decorations. Stay with your group, keep a drink in sight, and bow out when you want. A small drink is optional. Soft drinks and mocktails count. Passing is always fine.

Order follows Uber Canada's 2026 Nightlife Index, as reported by Daily Hive and TodoCanada. It tracks late-night ride demand, not a best-party prize.

You must be of legal drinking age in your location.
